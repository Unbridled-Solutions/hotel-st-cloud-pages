The Night

A Holiday Tradition in the Making

The evening begins aboard a festive bus winding through Cañon City's neighborhoods, where front porches glow, rooftops sparkle, and entire blocks compete for holiday bragging rights. Christmas music fills the ride as you take in some of the city's most creative displays, from timeless classics to displays that can only be described as enthusiastic.

When the tour comes to an end, the evening continues at Hotel St. Cloud. The lobby will be dressed for the season, hot cocoa and holiday treats will be waiting, and Santa will be making an appearance. Snap a family photo, drop a letter in Santa's mailbox, and enjoy a little extra Christmas magic before heading home. It's the kind of holiday evening that feels increasingly rare: simple, festive, and designed to be shared.

Offered on Thursday, December 10 and Tuesday, December 22. With just 40 seats available each night, the experience stays cozy, comfortable, and intentionally small.
