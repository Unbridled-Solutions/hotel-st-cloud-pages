The night

Murder in Buffalo Chip

A railroad detective makes a stop in Buffalo Chip, Colorado. Nefarious business. Conflicting romantic interests. Gunslingers, outlaws, ranchers, holy rollers, and prospectors in a gala that is, for once, being held somewhere respectable.

You can skip the saloon. The shindig is here. Red Herring has been writing these rooms for forty years. We have hosted them in this building before. This year the script is new. The hotel is the same one that has been taking in strangers since 1883, which feels about right for a town called Buffalo Chip.

What to expect

  • Dinner at Hotel St. Cloud
