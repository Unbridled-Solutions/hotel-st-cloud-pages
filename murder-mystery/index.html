The night

Murder in Buffalo Chip

A railroad detective makes a stop in Buffalo Chip, Colorado. Conflicting interests, questionable motives, and a cast of gunslingers, ranchers, holy rollers, prospectors, and outlaws have all gathered for a gala that is, for once, being held somewhere respectable. Before the evening is over, someone will be dead, and the mystery will be yours to solve.

You can skip the saloon. The shindig is here. We've hosted Red Herring in these historic walls before, and this year they're returning with an all-new mystery. Gather at 1887 Historic Eatery for an evening of laughter, intrigue, and good company as the story unfolds around you.

The company

Red Herring Productions

For decades, Red Herring Productions has been creating interactive murder mystery events throughout Colorado. Known for their historically inspired characters, audience participation, and comedic storytelling, their productions invite guests to become part of the mystery rather than simply watch it unfold. We're thrilled to welcome them back to Hotel St. Cloud with an all-new Old West mystery.
