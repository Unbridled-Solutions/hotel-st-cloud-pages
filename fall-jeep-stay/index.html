The ride

Half a day in open country

Colorado Jeep Tours is locally owned, four generations deep in Cañon City. Their half-day Royal Gorge tour runs about 3.5 hours: check in, climb from town toward Fremont Peak, look into the gorge. This package includes tickets to the Royal Gorge Bridge.

Guides talk history, geology, and what you are actually looking at. Dogs are welcome on most tours. You do not need to be an off-road person. You need a jacket, a camera, and a little time.

The stay

Then we take it from there

We have 36 rooms in one of Colorado's ten oldest hotels, restored as a 4-star house on Main Street. After the tour you are not driving back to a chain on the highway. You are two blocks from dinner, a cocktail, and a marble shower.

This is the living room of Cañon City. Come in dusty. We will still be glad to see you.
