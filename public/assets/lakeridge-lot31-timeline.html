Critical Path
🔴
Order Your Log Package and Glass Wall System on Day 1

Full-round log packages from mountain suppliers carry 12–16 week lead times. Custom glass wall systems (22-ft × 2-story) run 16–20 weeks from fabrication order. These two items are your critical path — they must be ordered at the same time as permit submittal, or your shell crew sits idle and the whole schedule slips 3–4 months. Don't wait for the permit to approve before ordering. Work with your GC to place conditional orders.

1 · Pre-Construction & Team Assembly
M1–M2
  • Engage architect; finalize schematic → design development
  • Hire structural engineer (snow load + hillside + glass wall)
  • Commission geotechnical investigation report (required by Grand County)
  • Hire GC — get bids, check mountain references
  • Resolve 2½-story county confirmation with Grand County
  • Finalize lot-line easement with Lot 32 neighbor
  • Begin STR compliance research (Town of Grand Lake + county dual licenses)
⚠️ Lock trades 6–12 months before you need them. Mountain crews book out fast.
2 · Design Development & Permitting
M2–M5
  • Submit building permit application to Grand County (target before M3)
  • Plan review: allow 4–8 weeks minimum; call 970-725-3255 for current wait list
  • Structural stamp required — 100+ PSF snow load, hillside retaining, 22-ft glass wall
  • Complete ~170 sf trim pass to stay under 4,100 sf cap
  • Fire impact fee: ~$1.26–$1.52/sf for Grand Fire Protection District
  • HOA water tap: $5,000 fee + $780/yr (already confirmed)
  • Engineered septic permit (Grand County perc rarely passes — assume engineered system)
3+4 · Critical Lead-Time Orders
M2–M7
  • Log Package: Full-round log package — 12–16 week lead time from mountain suppliers. Place conditional order at permit submittal, NOT after approval.
  • Glass Wall System: 22-ft × 2-story custom curtain wall — 16–20+ weeks fabrication lead. Structural engineering drawings required to place order.
  • 56-ft deck window/slider systems: order same window package
  • Residential elevator: 3-stop shaft rough-in must be designed into foundation; elevator cab delivery 12–16 weeks
🔴 These are your schedule killers. Delay here = 3-4 month project slip.
5 · Site Prep & Excavation
M4–M6
  • Access road buildout for equipment (45% grade = specialized equipment)
  • Tree clearing and defensible space establishment per Grand County fire rules
  • 3-level stepped excavation into hillside (major cut — 105 ft vertical fall)
  • Geotechnical monitoring during excavation (required for cut slopes)
  • Cut slope ratio: 3:1 per Grand County hillside requirements
  • Retaining wall engineered design and installation
  • Utility trenching: electrical, gas, septic, HOA water tie-in
  • Septic system installation (engineered — plan for $35K–$50K)
⚠️ 45% grade = highest excavation cost tier. Get 3 local bids; first quotes will vary wildly.
6 · Foundation (3 Levels)
M6–M8
  • 48-inch frost depth required in Grand County — full perimeter
  • Stepped 3-level foundation tied into hillside grade
  • Elevator shaft foundation designed and poured monolithically
  • Dumbwaiter chase framing coordinated with MEP rough
  • Moisture barrier and perimeter drainage system
  • Target all pours May–September to avoid freeze risk
⚠️ Foundation exposure: Grand County may limit to max 36" above grade on downhill face — confirm with architect.
7 · Log Shell Erection
M7–M11
  • Crane mobilization (steep access — specialty lift equipment)
  • Full-round log wall installation, Level 3 (entry/garage) first
  • Level 2 (main living) — great room vaulted structure, 23-ft vault framing
  • Level 1 (lower walkout) construction
  • Bunk level rough shell framing above garage (unfinished)
  • All door/window bucks installed with settling space (logs settle 3–6" Year 1)
  • All mechanical penetrations sleeved for settling
  • Stair connections designed for log settling
  • Log chinking + 2-coat fire-rated stain (required by Grand County)
⚠️ Log settling: every door, window, stair, and MEP penetration must have 3–6" settling gap built in. Missing this = Year 1 cracking and binding.
8 · Roofing
M9–M11
  • Ice + water shield — mandatory Grand County (ice dams are real at 8,400 ft)
  • Black standing-seam metal roofing — multi-level complex geometry
  • Roof penetrations: elevator shaft, dumbwaiter, HRV, fireplace chimney
  • 56-ft deck roof structure integrated with main roofline
  • Snow guards on all eave lines (load management)
  • Close-in before first hard snow (target complete by M11/November)
9 · Exterior Stone & Cladding
M10–M12

Grand Lake · Location-Specific Watch Items
🔴 Lead Times Are Your Biggest Risk
Log packages run 12–16 weeks. Custom glass wall systems run 16–20 weeks. Order both at permit submittal — not after approval. A 2-month delay here costs 3–4 months on the back end.
🔴 45% Grade — Budget for the Unexpected
Three-level stepped excavation in granite hillside terrain. Costs are hard to estimate without a geotech report. Your first excavation bid and your third can differ by 40%. Get all three before committing.
⚠️ 2½-Story Confirmation
Still listed as an open item. Resolve with Grand County before design development locks. This affects floor plate design, window wall proportions, and permit-ability of the bunk level.
⚠️ Log Settling — Plan for Year 1 Revisit
Full-round green logs settle 3–6 inches in Year 1. Every door, window, stair connection, and mechanical penetration needs a settling gap designed in. Budget a Year 1 callback visit with your GC to address settling adjustments.
⚠️ Concrete Windows: May–September Only
Grand County's hard freeze comes early and stays late. All concrete pours must land in the May–September window. Miss it, and foundation work pushes to the following spring — adding 5–6 months to the schedule.
⚠️ Snow Load — Structural Stamp Non-Negotiable
Grand County enforces 100+ PSF roof design load at this elevation. A licensed structural engineer's stamp is required. Budget $20K–$45K for structural engineering scope that includes the glass wall, deck cantilever, and hillside retaining.
⚠️ Engineered Septic — Budget It In
Grand County soils at this elevation rarely pass conventional perc tests. Assume an engineered system: $35K–$50K + ~$850 permit fee. Get the septic design permitted on a parallel track to building permits.
⚠️ STR Dual Licensing
Grand Lake STRs require a Nightly Rental License from the Town AND potentially a Short-Term Rental Permit from Grand County, plus a Colorado Sales Tax License. Confirm jurisdiction for Lakeridge Lot 31. Start both applications as soon as the C of O issues — rental income revenue starts at licensing, not at CO.
