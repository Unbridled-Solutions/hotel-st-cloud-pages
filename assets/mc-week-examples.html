The call on the page

Non-op is honest only if it is not giving way or locking. BPC-157 is not a stitch for that picture. If he operates: fight to repair, revision ACL, quad or unused hamstring, skip BTB first because of the kneecap cartilage.

How to work with MC

Do this

  • Send the photo, the PDF, the voice memo, the number.
  • Name the change. One change. Then we ship.
  • Ask what MC recommends. You get a call, not a menu.
  • Open the live URL. That is the deliverable.

Skip this

  • Treating it like a smarter search box.
  • Asking for ten options when you wanted a decision.
  • Letting a late night lower the bar. Mediocrity is not a rounding error.
  • Shipping anything we would not frame.
