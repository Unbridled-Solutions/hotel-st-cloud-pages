Tax exempt rooms

Hotel St. Cloud front desk · Cañon City, Fremont County · 21 Sep 2026 · Full SOP: offers.hotelstcloud.com/assets/hsc-tax-exempt

Tax exempt does not mean every tax is zero. Qualify the entity, then the payment. If either fails, collect Colorado Tax and Occupation Tax. When in doubt, collect and give an itemized folio.

Taxable room ≈ 10.7% tax. Military 10% and edu/gov 7% are rates. Those guests still pay tax.

Who qualifies

  1. U.S. or Colorado government (county, city, school, special district) on official business, paid with government funds.
  2. Qualifying charity with Colorado 98 / 098 certificate (or qualifying out-of-state cert), paid with org funds. Over $250: org check or org card.
  3. 30 consecutive nights with a written agreement.
  4. Diplomatic OFM card.

Who does not

  • Military / veteran ID (use 10% rate)
  • Teacher or gov employee on a personal card
  • GSA Travel IBA (sixth digit 1–4)
  • Reimbursed later / contractor for the government
  • Club, chamber, trade association, 501(c)(4)/(c)(6)
  • IRS letter with no Colorado 98 / 098
  • OTA folio at check-in (manager / Jessica)

Payment is the gate

  • PO / direct bill, entity check, org card: can exempt
  • GSA Purchase (red) or Integrated (gold): CBA, can exempt
  • GSA Tax Advantage (silver, CBA Tax Exempt): room can exempt
  • GSA Travel, sixth digit 6–0: CBA, can exempt
  • GSA Travel, sixth digit 1–4: IBA, full tax
  • Colorado OneCard / 98-02565 business travel: can exempt
  • “FOR INDIVIDUAL BUSINESS TRAVEL ONLY”: full tax
  • Personal card even with a cert in the bag: full tax

Do not write the full card number. Note “Travel IBA digit 3” or “Travel CBA digit 8.”

Cloudbeds, every time

  1. Documents on the guest (not the reservation). Cert, card photo, PO, DR 5002.
  2. Guest status upper right: Tax Exempt.
  3. Book the earned rate. Tax exempt is not a rate plan.
  4. Folio → Posted and Pending. Colorado Tax $0 and Occupation Tax $0 on room.
  5. If tax is already on the folio: ADD/ADJUST CHARGE → Adjust Charge → Colorado Tax, then Occupation Tax. Do not adjust Room Rate. Refund the tax dollars if they already paid.
  6. Guest note: entity, cert number, official business, payment type, initials, date.
  7. F&B and incidentals stay taxed unless the same entity is paying those and they still qualify.
