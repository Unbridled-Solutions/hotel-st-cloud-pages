🎯 Unbridled Opportunity Take

Warmest leads with direct contacts: Novo Nordisk (Tara Semisch — named Director of Strategic Meeting & Event Management) and Sanofi (Regina Kallai — named Strategic Meeting Planner). These are the two companies to approach first.

Smaller biotechs (Celcuity, Vera, Praxis, Achieve) likely outsource events or handle them through comms. Greenfield territory — approach C-suite or communications leads directly and Unbridled can position as a full-service partner for their first major approval launch event.

🎯 What This Means for Unbridled

Every one of these companies is about to throw a launch party — literally. Commercial launches mean national sales force kickoffs, HCP speaker programs, advisory boards, incentive travel for top performers, and internal celebration events. The window right after FDA approval is when the budget is freshest and the energy is highest.

Highest-value targets: Eli Lilly (two obesity/diabetes launches), Novo Nordisk (three launches including CagriSema), AstraZeneca, Gilead, GSK, J&J, and Pfizer — all are large enough to run multi-million dollar launch programs and have established events infrastructure.

Emerging opportunities: Celcuity, Corcept, BeiGene, Orca Bio, Stemline — smaller biotechs with their first or second major approval. These teams are often scrappier, less tied to incumbent agencies, and more open to a values-driven partner like Unbridled.
