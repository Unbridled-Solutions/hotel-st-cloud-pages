Whole Floor Shut Off

  1. Go to the housekeeping closet on the affected floor.
  2. Locate the water heater — find valves labeled "Hot Water Shut Off" and "Water Shut Off."

Electric Panel & Shut Off

Breakers · room outages · full building outage
▼
Electrical Contact Flowright 📞 719-564-2101 (press 1) 🚨 911 (emergency)

Panel Key & Locations

  • Panel key is in the front desk safe, labeled "Electric Panels."
  • Each floor has two breaker boxes: one near the elevator (rooms 1–6) and one at the end of the hall (rooms 7–12).
  • Each panel has an interior directory showing which breaker corresponds to which room.

Reset a Tripped Breaker

  1. Locate the correct breaker box using the room number and floor (elevator = rooms 1–6, hallway end = rooms 7–12).
  2. Open with the panel key from the front desk safe.
  3. Find the breaker: ON = fully left. OFF = fully right. TRIPPED = stuck in the middle.
  4. To reset tripped breaker: flip it fully OFF first → wait 5 seconds → flip it ON.
  5. If problem persists: notify manager and consider relocating the guest.
Tripped breaker (stuck in middle). Flip fully OFF first, wait, then flip ON.
Normal breaker positions: fully left = ON, fully right = OFF.

Full Building Outage — While It's Out

  1. Call Black Hills Energy: 888-890-5554 — to check for a known utility outage in the area.
  2. Confirm no one is trapped in the elevator. (Elevator auto-returns to the nearest floor below on battery backup.)
  3. Pull a screenshot of in-house guest list from Cloudbeds.
  4. If outage lasts more than 10 minutes: message all in-house guests via Cloudbeds.
  5. If elevator issues arise: call MEI Dispatch: 719-344-2814.

Full Building Outage — After Power Restored

  • Wait 10 minutes before using the elevator.
  • Reignite all kitchen appliances (see Gas → Reignite Kitchen Appliances).
  • Walk each floor to confirm lights, HVAC, and room systems are back online.
  • Notify manager that restoration is complete.

Gas Leak — How to Identify & Respond

Natural gas is odorized with mercaptan — it smells like rotten eggs or sulfur. If you detect this smell anywhere inside the building, treat it as a gas leak until confirmed otherwise.

  1. Do NOT turn any switches on or off — even a light switch can create a spark.
  2. Alert everyone nearby calmly and begin evacuating the building.
  3. Leave doors open as you exit to help ventilate.
  4. Once outside and clear of the building: call 911.
  5. Then call Atmos Energy: 888-286-6700.
  6. Do NOT re-enter until cleared by the fire department or Atmos Energy.
⚠️
Note — elevator area: A faint gas smell near the elevator on the first floor has been noted previously. This should always be reported and investigated. Do not dismiss it as normal — treat it as a potential leak until Atmos Energy confirms otherwise.

Reignite Kitchen Appliances (After Power Outage)

  1. Go to the prep line and find the hood vent screen on the ceiling — it is located above the cooking line toward the back of the kitchen. Look for the control panel mounted on the underside of the hood.
  2. Press the top right button to open the gas lines to the kitchen.
  3. Confirm the green gas handle behind each machine is in the ON position (parallel to the pipe).
  4. Use a lighter to light the pilot on each appliance:
    • Grill: light each section and confirm all ignite.
    • Stovetop: pilots are under the grates — accessible from the front.
    • Fryer: the blue pilot light indicator is located on the front face of the unit — confirm it is lit before use.

▼
Plumbing Contact Flowright 📞 719-564-2101 (press 1)

Water Leak

  1. Identify the source and close the nearest pipe valve (see Water Shut Off Guide above).
  2. Notify manager.
  3. Relocate affected guests if necessary.

Drain Blockage — Sink or Shower

Snakes are in the hobbit closet near the elevator on the first floor. Small plastic snakes = sinks/showers.

  1. Remove the drain cover with a screwdriver.
  2. Insert the small plastic snake and work it through to dislodge the blockage.

Drain Blockage — Toilet

Toilet auger (long metal snake) stored in the trash bag in the basement at the office across the street — 110 N 7th, behind Standard Oil.

  1. Insert the auger as far as possible into the toilet drain.
  2. Spin the handle in circles to dislodge the blockage.
⚠️
Do NOT push the auger to the bottom if it extends far without resistance. An object may be lodged and allowing the snake to pass. Pushing to the bottom will break the toilet's wax seal. Stop and contact management.

Individual Room Unit Not Working

  1. Check that the thermostat in the room is set correctly and the unit has power.
  2. Check the breaker for that room (see Electric panel guide).
  3. If still not working: notify manager and offer the guest a room change if available.

Building-Wide HVAC Issue

  1. Note which floors or areas are affected.

  4. While waiting: open windows where possible for ventilation, notify manager, and update affected guests.

Person Trapped in Elevator

🚨
Call 911 immediately. Do not attempt to open the elevator doors manually.

  3. Call elevator territory manager Billy Carroll: 719-505-2658 or billy.carroll@meiusa.com

Elevator During Power Outage

  • Confirm the elevator is empty and no one is trapped.

How monitoring works · alarm panel · emergency phones

Fire / Emergency Fire Department 🚨 911

How Our Fire Alarm Monitoring System Works

Hotel St. Cloud is equipped with an Edwards fire alarm system that is actively monitored 24/7 by a third-party central monitoring station. When the alarm is triggered:

  • The monitoring station is automatically notified and will contact the fire department if they cannot reach a hotel representative to confirm a false alarm.
  • If the alarm is accidental (e.g. steam, cooking smoke, a test), call the monitoring station immediately with your PIN to cancel the dispatch. The monitoring number and PIN are posted on or near the alarm panel.
  • If you cannot reach the monitoring station in time, the fire department will be dispatched — allow them to clear the building before resuming operations.
  • Never silence an alarm without first identifying the cause. Use the ACK/Panel Silence button only after you have confirmed the source and it is not a real fire.

Alarm Panel — Location & Controls

  • The Edwards fire alarm control panel is located near the elevator on the first floor.
  • System Record Documents are posted to the left of the panel.
  • The panel display shows the property name and address: "Hotel St Cloud, 631 Main St."
  • ACK/Panel Silence button: acknowledges the alarm and silences the panel horn. Does NOT cancel the monitoring station notification — call them separately.
Fire alarm control panel (Edwards) — located near the elevator. System Record Documents are posted to the left.
Alarm panel display — shows "Hotel St Cloud, 631 Main St." Arrow points to ACK/Panel Silence button.

Emergency Communication Phones

  • The front desk emergency communication panel has floor-by-floor indicator lights and a red emergency handset — use it to communicate with guests on any floor during an alarm.
  • Stairwell emergency phones are located on each floor (2nd floor lobby and above) — push the button for help. These are for guests who need evacuation assistance.

Evacuation Protocol Procedure

Assembly point · guest & staff headcount · DO NOT list
▼
🚨
Evacuate FIRST. Account for everyone SECOND. Do not delay evacuation to gather belongings, silence alarms, or check systems. Every second counts — get everyone out.
Step 1

Call 911. Give the address: 631 Main St, Cañon City, CO 81212.

Step 2

Activate the fire alarm if not already triggered. Alert all floors verbally as you evacuate — knock on doors and announce loudly as you move through each floor.

Step 3

Assembly point: Standard Oil Coffee Co. — 110 N 7th St, across 7th Street from the hotel. Direct everyone there immediately.

Step 4

Pull the in-house guest list from Cloudbeds on your phone and run a headcount once assembled at Standard Oil.

Step 5

Pull the staff-on-duty list from Sling and confirm all staff are accounted for.

Step 6

Report headcount status to the 911 dispatcher and the on-call manager. Do not re-enter the building until cleared by the fire department.

📍
Assembly point: Standard Oil Coffee Co. — 110 N 7th St, across 7th Street from the hotel (not behind it). Keep everyone together and well away from the building. Do not allow anyone to linger near the entrance.

🚫 During Evacuation — DO NOT

  • Do NOT use the elevator under any circumstances during a fire alarm or evacuation.
  • Do NOT re-enter the building for any reason until the fire department gives the all-clear.
  • Do NOT attempt to retrieve guest belongings, equipment, or personal items.
  • Do NOT silence the fire alarm before evacuating — the alarm is protecting people.
  • Do NOT prop open stairwell doors — they are fire-rated and must remain closed to contain smoke and flames.
  • Do NOT allow guests to linger in the lobby or near the building entrance — move everyone across 7th Street to Standard Oil.
  • Do NOT assume a false alarm until confirmed by the fire department. Treat every alarm as real.

Guests Needing Evacuation Assistance

  • Guests who cannot use the stairs should be directed to the stairwell landing on their floor — this is a fire-rated refuge area.
  • Use the stairwell emergency phone on that floor to notify the front desk and emergency services of their location.
  • A staff member should stay on the landing with the guest if it is safe to do so, until fire department personnel can assist.
