Question 3 of 7
Protect & Uphold Values
4 / 5 — Consistently performs beyond expectations

You consistently advocate for and reinforce our company decisions, and you're not afraid to come to me and debate when something feels off or doesn't hold up to our values. I appreciate that. You communicate well on the company's behalf, collaborate across teams, and you're accountable. You actively embrace direction and vision, and that shows.

I rated you a four because there are values-based decisions I want us to slow down on and contemplate more carefully. UnbridledACTS is the generosity output for Unbridled. We mobilize resources, people, and funding toward causes that represent the heart of our people. But sometimes those causes can conflict, or potentially conflict, with our core values of character, change, credibility, and community. And sometimes they can put us in compromising situations.

The awareness I want you to carry is that we will occasionally find ourselves at the crossroads of political or hot-button topics that we'd rather avoid or navigate carefully. Our core mission is helping the human condition, directly, one-on-one, peer-to-peer, mobilizing money to programs that impact individuals. A recycling program helps the human condition at some level, but it's not the purest form of what we're called to do. Similarly, we wouldn't want to give to an organization if the funds were offsetting administrative costs rather than reaching people directly.

When we face decisions that land at the crosshairs, I want us to talk about it. Hold it up to the light. Our desire to say yes should always be accompanied by the awareness that that yes may set us on a course we may or may not want to go down.
