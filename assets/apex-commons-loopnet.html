About APEX Commons
"Rare executive office space in the most desirable block on Main Street — fully restored, move-in ready, and priced to compete."

APEX Commons at 425 Main Street is Cañon City's premier historic office address. Located in the heart of downtown between 4th and 7th Street — the most sought-after commercial corridor in Fremont County — this fully renovated second-floor property offers 23 private office suites in a beautifully restored late 19th-century building.

The building has been gut-renovated to modern standards while preserving its historic character: high ceilings, large windows with natural light, and quality finishes throughout. Suites range from compact 123 SF executive offices to spacious 303 SF corner suites, with both window-facing and interior configurations available.

Tenants enjoy access to shared amenities including a conference room, break room, open co-working area, and updated restrooms — all included in a simple, all-in full-service gross lease. No triple-net surprises. No add-ons. One monthly payment covers everything.

Whether you're a solo practitioner, a small firm, a regional office, or a startup looking for a professional Main Street address in Colorado's scenic Fremont County, APEX Commons delivers rare quality at a price point that still makes sense.
