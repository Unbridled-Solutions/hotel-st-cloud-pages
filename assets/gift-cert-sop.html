What this is

A dollar-value stay credit. We collect once (or issue a free night) and apply it at booking. Do not sell a third-party gift card. Do not start a Google Sheet. The generator is the list.

Issue a paid gift

  1. Copy the next HSCG code from the generator. Do not Save or Print yet.
  2. Cloudbeds → House Accounts → Gift Certificates. Never + New House Account per sale.
  3. Add/Adjust Charge → Add Item.
  4. Category Gift Certificates. Product Hotel Gift Certificate.
  5. Notes: HSCG2026-050 - Jane Smith (code + recipient). Amount = this sale only.
  6. Phone: stay on the call. Add Payment → Pay by Link → Charge. Walk them to the email. Folio shows Payment from Pay by Link.
  7. In person: Add Payment → Terminal. Run the card. Never send a link to someone at the counter.
  8. Money is in. Generator: fill the form, Pay Link Sent → Yes, Save, Print. Then Clear.
  9. Email from reservations@, or hand the voucher at the desk.

Issue complimentary (HSCC)

Manager yes first. Generator → Complimentary → recipient, amount, optional org/event, optional Expires on, Approved by Lorin/Kim/Lindsay → Save → Print. No Pay by Link.

Redeem

  1. Ask for the code. Search the generator if they lost the paper.
  2. Confirm it is Open and check remaining dollars.
  3. Create the reservation. Payment method Gift Certificate. Amount = this stay only. Collect leftover cash if the stay is more.
  4. If the stay is less, ask: keep leftover on the cert, or spend on F&B this stay. F&B leftover is a second Gift Certificate payment on the reservation folio. Notes: {code} leftover F&B.
  5. Paste the code into reservation notes.
  6. Generator → Apply / Redeem. Amount this stay, leftover choice, reservation ID, agent name, note checkbox. Remaining $0 becomes Redeemed. Else it stays Open with leftover.

Do not send Pay by Link a second time at stay.

Void

Void keeps the record (name + reason: test, duplicate, accident) and drops it off Open. Codes stay reserved. Restore if you voided by mistake. Cannot redeem a voided cert until Restore.

Do not

  • Gift Up, VoucherCart, or plastic gift cards
  • A new House Account per buyer
  • Pay by Link for someone standing at the counter
  • Expiry dates on paid certs
  • Lindsay’s email on the voucher
  • Mint the next code on Save
