What I would not do

  • Do not copy the 2026 file’s 75% summer occupancy. You have not printed it on 36 keys.
  • Do not put hotel labor inside the 65% prime. You were clear. Keep it on rooms. Then staff the desk to a number, not a leftover.
  • Do not hold 33% F&B labor as a hope. Write the weekly labor planners to it or it will not happen. 2026 P1–P9 was 43%.
  • Do not staff P1–P4 or P12–P13 to the percent model. The $110k period floor is the winter house. Below that occupancy, labor does not work.
  • Do not scale this to 60/66 keys yet. This is the 36-key house you will actually operate in 2027.
