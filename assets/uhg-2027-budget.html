MC’s read

  1. Do not build 2027 hotel occupancy off 2025 + 10%. The hotel was not a real year in 2025. May–Sep 2025 ran ~17–45% occ. 2026 P1–P9 already ran 41% at $205 ADR. 2025 + 10% would budget fewer rooms than you are already selling.
  2. Use 2026 occupancy × 1.10, ADR × 1.05. That is the 10% / 5% you want, on a house that exists. Cap any period at 85%. Do not put 75% back on P8–P11. 2026 budgeted 53.8% for the year. P1–P9 actual was 41%. The miss is occupancy, not rate.
  3. Prime at 65% exclusive of hotel is a F&B target, not a trend. P1–P9 food is already ~30% of F&B. Labor on F&B is 43% with tax. 32% food is free. 33% labor is a 10-point cut. That is the whole job.
  4. Hotel labor is outside that 65% and it is heavy. P1–P9 hotel labor with tax is 46% of room revenue. Rooms do not cover the desk + $370–$534 management stack. If hotel labor stays there, 2027 NOI is roughly zero after rent, FF&E, and G&A. If hotel labor gets to 30% of rooms, NOI is about $190k.
  5. 1887 is carrying the box. P1–P9: 1887 ~$1.09M, FP ~$620k + $81k events, SOCC ~$231k, rooms ~$761k. Lift 1887 with occupied rooms (half in-house). Leave FP and coffee on their own pace — they are not hotel-driven.
