How I see 2027

A note to our live event partners  ·  October 2026

Dear live event partners,

This is a season of change.

Once a year we sit down together to forecast and plan the year ahead. Too often that room has been about numbers, profits, revenue, and raises. Those things matter. They are not the whole job. For 2027 I want us more strategic than that. I want us aligned on a few key initiatives, and on a way of thinking that can actually guide the year.

This letter is how I see it. It is not carved in stone. It is an invitation to think together, not a decree from on high.

The cornerstone that feels most relevant to me for 2027 is Change. We do not set the other three down to pick it up. Character, Credibility, and Community still hold. We just do not get to pretend this year is a straight line.

Deliver Exceptional.

Those two phrases still sit at the center. Exceptional is the work in the room. World class is the company we are building so that work can happen again, without heroics. Under them are six pillars. Each one is a commitment, not a slogan.

For every pillar I want to say three things. How I see it. What it could be. What we need to do to get there. I will narrate those as we go. Until then, the boxes stay open on purpose.
