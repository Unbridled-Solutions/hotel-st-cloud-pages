Most Popular

Fixed Index Annuity (FIA)

Your principal is protected. When the market goes up, you participate. When it drops, you stay at zero — never negative.

  • Principal protection — floor of zero
  • Growth tied to market index (S&P 500)
  • Guaranteed lifetime income rider option
  • Tax-deferred growth
  • No direct market exposure
💰
Best for Lump Sums

Multi-Year Guaranteed Annuity (MYGA)

Like a CD — but with rates that beat most banks. Lock in a guaranteed rate for 3, 5, or 7 years. Simple and predictable.

  • Guaranteed fixed rate for term length
  • Better rates than most CDs
  • Tax-deferred growth
  • No market tracking needed
  • Ideal for rollover or settlement funds
📅
For Immediate Income

Income Rider / SPIA

Need income now? Turn your lump sum into a guaranteed monthly check — starting immediately or at a future date you choose.

  • Predictable monthly income
  • Income you can't outlive
  • Protects against longevity risk
  • Spouse continuation options
  • Ideal after divorce settlement or inheritance

Florida Advantage

Florida women have unique advantages.
Most advisors don't talk about them.

Where you live matters. Florida has specific laws that make annuities even more powerful as a retirement tool.

No State Income Tax

Annuity income in Florida is not subject to state income tax — more of every payment stays in your pocket.

Creditor Protection

Florida law provides strong protection for annuity assets from creditors — a meaningful advantage if you're protecting a settlement or inheritance.

Senior Consumer Protections

Florida Statute 627.4554 provides specific protections for buyers 65+ — including limits on surrender charges. We always work within these rules.

Best Interest Standard

Florida requires advisors to act in your best interest — not theirs. We hold ourselves to that standard before the law requires it.
