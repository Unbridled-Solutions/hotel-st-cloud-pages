  • Solutions assigns 6% and 5% now. No check from them.

Option 3 — skip membership for now (answers the flinching partner)

Profit share now. Membership later, or never, person by person.

This is the flexibility Stan asked for, and it is also the cleanest answer to “too much work to value and transfer shares.”

  • Economic only: 6% and 5% of the 60% shareholder distribution (after 20% Acts and 20% retained), as a contractual share. No units. No new members. No full OA rewrite. They get paid like owners. They do not vote like owners.
  • Split the pair: Liz takes membership (managing partner actually needs authority). Stephanie stays profits-only if she does not want another K-1 / OA fight. Or the reverse.
  • Convert later: profits interest converts to units after 24 months, or when the note would have been retired, or when the partner group is ready to amend.

Partner group likes more partners in the abstract and resists in the particular. A profits share does not force the group to reopen the whole operating agreement on day one. Leadership still changes on Monday.

Other ways deals like this get done

  1. Profits interest (if GIFT'D is a partnership for tax). Grant a profits interest in future appreciation only. Often $0 tax if an 83(b) is filed in 30 days and the $200k is a real capital account. This is the “gift without a taxable event” people actually mean. Counsel has to confirm entity tax status.
  2. Reverse vesting. Units or profits sit in their name on day one, unvest over 3–4 years (or vest as the note pays down). Quit or get fired for cause: unvested returns to Solutions. This protects the partner group more than a pure gift.
  3. Two-class: vote vs money. Liz gets managing-member authority (or an officer appointment + budget rights) even if her economic % is 6%. Do not confuse “equity partner” with “can run the place.” Karla can keep 19% economics and lose the managing seat.
  4. Phantom equity. Cash bonus equal to 5%/6% of a defined profit. No units, no K-1 as a member. Easiest for the resistant partner. Weakest as “they own it.”
  5. Earn-in on performance, not just time. Half the 6%/5% for showing up as MP / sales lead. Other half when turnover, NPS / client retention, or contribution margin hit a floor. Stops a feel-good grant if the turnaround never happens.
