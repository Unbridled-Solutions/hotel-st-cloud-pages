The building

Built as a post office. Used as a hall.

The Post Office went up in 1931. Italian Renaissance Revival mixed with Beaux Arts: terra cotta, Spanish tile, arches, original terrazzo, marble, millwork, and the letterboxes. It has been on the National Register of Historic Places since 1985. We renovated it as an event center. The bones stayed.
