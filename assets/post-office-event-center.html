You are not renting a hall. You are stepping into the post office.

This is where Cañon City used to wait for news from far away. The boxes are still on the wall. Come get married in them.

The building

People came here to hear from the ones they loved.

In the early 1900s, the post office was the center of town. Large town, small town, medium town: this is where you came to be reconnected. Letters. Packages. Word that someone was coming home. That atmosphere is still in the room.

The letterboxes are original. So is the wear. Scratches on the doors. Divots in the floor from everyone who walked in to pick up the mail. We sourced marble from local quarries to match what was already here. High on the walls, the movies filmed in Cañon City. You are not decorating a blank box. You are walking into beautifully preserved history, from the years when a post office actually mattered.

Then we restored it soup to nuts. New roof. Copper gutters. Copper on the facade. Paint. HVAC. A building like this would cost millions upon millions to raise again today. Do not discount it as “just a post office.” This is a very special building, kept on purpose.
