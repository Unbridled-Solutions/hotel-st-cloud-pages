The town

Main Street in the morning. Red rock by afternoon.

Cañon City sits at the mouth of the Royal Gorge. Brick, river, and sky. You do not have to leave downtown to eat, sleep, or throw a night. When you do leave, the country starts in minutes.

The halls

A 1931 post office. A 1903 dance hall over Main.

Weddings, holiday parties, meetings, gallery nights, the kind of dinner that needs a room of its own. Both halls take about 150. Tables, chairs, linens, and the cleaning come with the room. Food is ours unless the Post Office night wants a kitchen from outside.

A day out

Jeeps. A gold-medal river. A train through the gorge.

You do not have to program the whole weekend. These are the days groups actually take from here. We will help you put a stay around them.

Jeep the gorge

Colorado Jeep Tours runs half-days through the red country. Dust, overlooks, and a guide who already knows the roads. We have built stays around this before.

Fish with Royal Gorge Anglers

The Arkansas is right here. Royal Gorge Anglers will put your people on the water, walk-and-wade or a boat, whatever the group can actually do.

Ride the train

The Royal Gorge Route leaves town and hangs over the river. Lunch, dinner, or a coach with a window. Come back to Main Street when the light goes.
