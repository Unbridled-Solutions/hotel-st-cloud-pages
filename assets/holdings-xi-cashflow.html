Related-party truth

Media at $30 is a $65k subsidy

What I would do, in order

1. Confirm the leftover bay and list it this month. Measure the unused SF. Price it as flex, not trophy office: $11.50–$16 NNN or $16–$19 modified, plus CAM. 3,200 SF at the neighbor’s $11.50 already clears 1.25x.
2. Do not renew Media down to $19.50 on more SF until the bay is leased. If Media needs relief, trade dollars for a shorter term or a real CAM true-up, not empty square footage.
3. Protest the $96k tax. That is $6.37 / SF and 30% of rent. A 25% win is +$24k cash and still not enough alone. Do it anyway. St. Louis County commercial is assessed at 32% of market. Get the card.
4. Pull the note. I reverse-fit 4.5% / $1.73M / $12,460 P&I from the interest schedule. If you are interest-only, cash is actually +$67k and this conversation is different. If you are amortizing, the sheet is hiding the principal.
5. Stop treating related-party rent as market occupancy. LBN + Media + Solutions can keep the lights on. A third-party check is what makes the building a real asset.
