624 Trade Center. Rent roll and tax.

From Stan · Lindsay to send · August 26, 2026

Team,

We had MC survey the Chesterfield market for 624 Trade Center. The Class B office asking rate in that area is about $23 a square foot ($23.73 in the 2025 Yardi / CommercialCafe set). That is the number we would qualify for if we were renting this kind of space as a user. We are using $23.

Then we got curious about the rent rolls. What should Solutions and Media even pay if we common-rated their space, same building, same kind of use? That query came back at $23 a square foot. About 75 cents under the Class B average. We also asked what if we wanted a 10% return on our money, which is just smart business. Same $23 still held. So we are looking at applying $23 to those new rents and seeing if that is the right rent roll.

Red flag: our property tax is way overstated. We have $96,000 a year on the 2027 sheet. That is $6.37 a square foot and 30% of rent. Contest it. St. Louis County commercial is assessed at 32% of market. A 25% win takes tax to about $72,000 and puts about $24,000 back in cash. Do it anyway. Get the card.
