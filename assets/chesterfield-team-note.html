624 Trade Center. Rent roll and tax.

From Stan · Lindsay to send · August 26, 2026

Team,

We had MC survey Chesterfield for 624 Trade Center. Class B office asking in that area is about $23 a square foot. The 2025 Yardi / CommercialCafe number is $23.73. So $23 sits about 75 cents under the average, which is the kind of space we would qualify for as a user.

Then we got curious about the rent rolls. What should Solutions and Media pay if we common-rated their space, same building, same kind of use? That came back at $23. We also asked what if we wanted a 10% return on our money, which is just smart business. Same $23 still held. So we are looking at applying $23 to those new rents.

Red flag: property tax looks way overstated. The 2027 sheet has $96,000. That is $6.37 a square foot and 30% of rent. Contest it. St. Louis County commercial is assessed at 32% of market. A 25% win takes tax to about $72,000 and puts about $24,000 back in cash. Do it anyway. Get the card.
