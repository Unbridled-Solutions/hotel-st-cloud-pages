How we actually do wine & cheese

Call it Pour & Plate, not a tasting. Locals come if it is cheap to understand, timed for real life, and over in 90 minutes.

The formula

Thursday 5:30–7:00 · $35

  • Four wines. A real board. Bread, honey, nuts.
  • One host. Eight minutes of talking, not forty.
  • Lead with Holy Cross Abbey. Locals show up for the monks’ wine on Main Street.
  • Board has to look like $35. Tiny cubes on toothpicks kill it.
  • End with a fork: kitchen is open, or The War Room is pouring.
Ops

Use 1887 or the lobby

  • Kitchen already on. One extra person on the board.
  • First two can be walk-in. Then prepaid once we know it sells.
  • Flyer line: “$35. Thursday 5:30. Four wines. A real board. Done at 7. Text us to hold a spot.”

The Rendezvous

10 people. 5 tables. A 3-course dinner in The War Room, which you normally cannot do. You leave with a numbered token. That is the whole product.

The product
$99

All-in. Service included. No math at the end. 3 courses, 3 wine pours, smoked cocktail moment, token, wax-sealed menu.

Rules that make it feel rare
  • 5 two-tops. No combining into a 10-top.
  • Prepaid. No walk-ups. No “my cousin might come.”
  • War Room closed to walk-ins 5:30–8. Sign on the door is the marketing.
  • One menu. Allergy only. Then we get the bar back.
  • Kitchen should feel like 1887 on a good night, not a tasting-menu lecture.
Never add a sixth table because we can squeeze them. Scarcity is the product. Closing The War Room on a slow Wednesday or Thursday is the trade. Do not run this on a Friday we are already busy, and skip Art Festival weekend (Sep 5–7).

The thing they take home

Do not give every guest a drink smoker. Use one smoker tableside. They remember the smoke. We keep the tool.

Must-do

The Token

Numbered brass coin. RENDEZVOUS on the front. Night number + year on the back. About $3–5 each in a 50-piece run. Pocketable. This is the “show your friends” object.

Must-do

Wax-sealed menu

One page. Hotel seal. They take it. Paper and wax. Looks like a letter from 1883.

Nice

Matchbook

War Room mark. About $1–2. Smokers and non-smokers both keep matchbooks.

Season mechanic without a “club”: Night 1 brass, Night 2 copper, winter black. After 3 tokens they get the first text when we release dates. We are not selling a membership. We are selling bragging rights and a head start.

Money on one Rendezvous night

10 guests. Use what we already stock. Keep labor skinny. Break-even is roughly 6–7 prepaid seats. That is why no-shows cannot be free.

Signup: make it stupid

  1. Text the hotel number. One word. One date.
  2. We reply with a Square or Toast prepaid link. No “we’ll get you back.”
  3. Waitlist in a note on the phone. Cancel → text the next name.
  4. Sold-out post the same day. Full is advertising.
