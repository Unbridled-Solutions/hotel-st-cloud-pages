Cloudbeds PIE

Turn occupancy rules automatic

This is Cloudbeds PIE, not a separate RMS. Occupancy rules change daily BAR. Derived plans (military 10%, edu 7%) move with BAR. If occupancy falls back, auto rules revert. Set min/max or PIE can run away.

Use percent, not dollars, so the ladder holds. Property occupancy on 36 rooms.

Min / max (STK; others in proportion)

  • In season: min $189 / max $399
  • Off season: min $159 / max $279

Do not auto-discount for low occupancy. Rural 4-star, 90% direct: cutting Tuesday to $139 does not create demand. It just lowers ADR.

Last-minute only, and only by hand for 30 days: if a date is 0–2 days out and occ under 25%, front office may go 10% off BAR, never below the season floor. If that actually fills rooms, then make it a PIE rule. Do not start there.

Compset in PIE: alerts only, never auto-follow. Holiday Inn Express / Best Western / Quality Inn will drag us down. Watch them. Do not match them.

Known Cloudbeds gap: group allotment blocks do not trigger occupancy rules until the rooming list is in. For Unbridled / wedding blocks, raise BAR by hand the day the block is loaded, or you will sell the rest of the house at the empty-Tuesday rate.

Guests ask because Marriott and Hilton trained them. Typical club deal is 5–10% (AAA) or 5–15% (AARP) at thousands of chain hotels.

Why no:

  1. On a $199 BAR, 10% is $20 a night. On 36 keys that becomes the new asking price the first week the desk says yes.
  2. We are independent. AAA/AARP is a distribution product for flags. We already have 90% direct.
  3. Two more rate plans, blackout logic, and “but I have AAA” at the desk. You asked for easy to manage.
  4. Value is already our weakest review score. Teaching people the real rate is 10% off fights the ADR goal.

Front desk script:

We’re an independent historic hotel, not a AAA or AARP property. We do 10% for military and veterans and 7% for teachers and government, with ID. If you’re staying two nights I can look at that.

If the asking gets unbearable later, the only acceptable version is one 5% “Explorer” code, direct only, blacked out Fri–Sat and any night ≥65% full. Still would not do it this year.

Never: stack discounts (military + AAA). Put club rates on Booking/Expedia. Let Expedia campaign on top of a discounted BAR (keep the existing Expedia +15% markup against 18% commission).

How to run it

Fifteen minutes, Mondays

  1. Open PIE → Rate Manager, next 21 days.
  2. Confirm auto rules fired on any night already ≥50%.
  3. Mark known demand: railroad, hunting, high school sports, FPH events, Unbridled blocks.
  4. Do not touch weekday floors unless it is 0–3 days out and truly empty.
  5. Once a season (May 1 / Nov 1): load the new floors. Stop.
