Section 2
Purpose · Hire 2.0.1

This is a loose, non-binding record of intent. It is written so Mike can see the room we are trying to make: two hires, not one.

The strategy is a US General Manager (Mike) and a sales manager (Kara) working alongside each other, inside a combined salary cap. Stan is less of a fan of a single GM hire. The cap exists so both seats can be filled without stacking two full standalone packages.

The commercial goal is revenue in the $2 million to $3 million range, then backfill production, build, and install. Kara's value is a consultative design-and-build approach and a white-glove walk through the entire build, not kit-only. The opportunity is a concierge path: navigate the whole process with the customer instead of only milling and delivering kits.

This document is ideation for Mike. It is not an employment contract. It does not grant equity. Either side can walk away.
