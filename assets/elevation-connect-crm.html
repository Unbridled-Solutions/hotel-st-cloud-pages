What else is in this space, and why this instead. Salesforce and HubSpot are the default BPO sales stack. Too much object model for a boutique pipeline. Pipedrive and Copper are the closest commercial cousins: three-stage deal, activity notes, phone on the contact. Five9 / Talkdesk add true dialer logging when you outgrow tap-to-call. We built this first because Chris named the convention (Company · scope · location), the three stages, and a four-quarter view. Those are not checkboxes in a generic CRM. Tap-to-call uses the phone on the record (tel:). When you want the call logged back into the note automatically, that is a Five9 or Twilio pass, not this first version.
