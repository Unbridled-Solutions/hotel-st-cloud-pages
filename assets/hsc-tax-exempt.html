Desk SOP for Hotel St. Cloud rooms in Cañon City, Fremont County. Written 21 Sep 2026 from Colorado DOR rooms guidance, governmental and charitable publications, DR 5002, GSA SmartPay Colorado notes, and our Cloudbeds names (Colorado Tax / Occupation Tax). Controller owns edge cases. If you cannot prove it, collect the tax.

The rule

Tax exempt does not mean every tax on the folio is zero. A badge, a 501(c) letter, military ID, or “the county is paying me back” is not enough. Two questions, in order:

  1. Is this guest (or the entity paying) actually exempt under Colorado law for rooms?
  2. Is the payment coming from that entity’s funds, not a personal card?

If either answer is no, collect every tax. Do not set Tax Exempt in Cloudbeds.

One-line version for the floor

Official government or qualifying charity, paid with their money, on a room stay under 30 days: we can drop Colorado Tax and Occupation Tax on the room. Personal travel, personal cards, military ID, school ID, and “we’re a nonprofit” without a Colorado 98 / 098 certificate: full tax. F&B and incidentals stay taxed unless the same entity is paying those too and they still qualify.

Our taxes

What is on a Hotel St. Cloud room

Cañon City is a state-collected city (not a self-collecting home-rule lodging city). Room tax is several layers. Cloudbeds shows two lines. Both can be exempt together, or both stay. Do not zero one and leave the other because it “sounds like occupancy.”

Cañon City sales tax went to 3.3% on 1 Jan 2025. An old Fremont County rate sheet still says 3.0% / 8.4%. Do not use that sheet for the desk.

Why the controller is right to worry

  • People say “tax exempt” when they mean a rate (military 10%, edu/gov 7%). Those guests still pay Colorado Tax and Occupation Tax.
  • Cloudbeds Tax Exempt on the guest can look done while a pending Occupation Tax line is still sitting on the folio. Always open Folio, Posted and Pending.
  • A charity certificate does not automatically kill tax on dinner, the minibar, or parking.
  • If we drop tax we cannot prove, the hotel owes it. Collect when it is fuzzy. They can take the receipt to Colorado DOR.

Who qualifies

1. Government on official business, paid by government

United States. State of Colorado. A Colorado county, city, school district, or special district. Their departments and agencies. Federal credit unions and Farm Credit Banks count with the U.S. list.

All of this must be true:

  • The stay is in a governmental capacity (work trip, not vacation).
  • The entity is the guest of record or the folio is billed to the entity.
  • Payment is a government check, warrant, purchase order / direct bill, or a centrally billed government card (see How they pay).

A Colorado exemption certificate (starts with 98 or 098) is helpful. Government does not have to have one. A signed DR 5002 plus the payment proof is enough.

2. Qualifying charity, paid by the charity

Colorado treats an organization as charitable for sales tax only if it is organized for religious, charitable, scientific, educational, literary, public-safety testing, amateur sports, or prevention of cruelty, and it does not lobby as a substantial part of its work. A 501(c)(3) letter is a starting point. Veterans orgs under 501(c)(19) also qualify.

Must have:

  • Colorado exemption certificate starting 98 / 098, verified on Revenue Online, or another state’s certificate and they still meet Colorado’s definition.
  • Stay is for their regular charitable work, not a staff vacation.
  • Paid from the organization’s funds. Over $250: organization check or organization credit card, not a volunteer’s personal card they will “get reimbursed.”

3. Thirty consecutive nights, written agreement

Colorado does not tax rooms rented to a permanent resident with a written agreement for at least 30 consecutive days. Hotel registration or a rent receipt counts. A canceled check alone does not. This also takes off state-administered local tax and county lodging tax. If someone extends to 30 days after arrival, stop charging room tax from the night the written 30-day agreement is in place. Ask a manager before you rewind earlier nights.

4. Diplomatic tax exemption card

U.S. Office of Foreign Missions card, presented at the desk. Copy front and back onto the guest. Same folio check as government.

Who does not

Collect full tax. Do not touch Tax Exempt.

  • Military, veteran, or DoD ID used for the 10% rate. That is a discount, not a tax exemption.
  • Teacher, school, or government employee on a personal trip, even with a work email. Use the 7% plan if they qualify. Tax stays.
  • GSA SmartPay individually billed travel card (see How they pay).
  • Another state’s city or county, paid on a personal or individually billed card.
  • Contractor, consultant, or vendor “working for the government.”
  • Guest will be reimbursed later. Reimbursement does not count. The money has to leave the entity now.
  • Country club, lodge, civic club, chamber, trade association, 501(c)(4) / (c)(6) “we’re nonprofit.”
  • Church or charity with only an IRS letter and no Colorado 98 / 098 (or qualifying out-of-state cert). Get the cert or collect tax.
  • AAA, AARP, resident of Cañon City, “I live here,” “I’m with the event.”
  • Meeting room with a bed in it. Banquet space without beds is not room tax. A suite used as a meeting is still a room.

Cloudbeds

Before you book, or before you take a card

  1. Qualify. Documents in hand. Payment method in hand. If either is missing, book as a normal stay. Tell them we can reverse tax when the packet is complete.
  2. Create / open the guest. Documents on the guest: upload the cert, card photo, PO, DR 5002.
  3. Upper right of the guest: status Tax Exempt.
  4. Book the room at the correct rate (BAR, government per diem, whatever they actually earned). Tax exempt is not a rate plan.
  5. Open Folio. Filter Posted and Pending. Colorado Tax and Occupation Tax must both be $0.00 on room revenue.
  6. If a tax line still has dollars, do not guess. Follow Folio check below.
  7. Note on the guest (not only the reservation): entity name, cert number, who authorized, official business, payment type (CBA / check / PO), your initials, date.

They already paid with tax on the folio

Only after the packet is complete:

  1. Documents on the guest. Status Tax Exempt.
  2. Folio → three lines so you see tax split: Colorado Tax and Occupation Tax.
  3. ADD/ADJUST CHARGE → Adjust Charge → Apply Adjustment to Colorado Tax → enter the amount currently on that line → ADJUST CHARGE.
  4. Repeat for Occupation Tax.
  5. Do not adjust Room Rate. You are not comping the room.
  6. If they paid by card, refund the tax dollars. Balance due must be $0 before they leave.
  7. Note what you reversed and why.

Master SOP used to say “if they booked on the phone you do not need to adjust.” That is only true if you already confirmed both tax lines are zero. Always look.

OTA / Booking.com / Expedia

Do not strip tax on an OTA folio because the guest flashed a badge at check-in. The channel already settled tax their way. Take the documents, note the profile, call a manager. Accounting decides if anything gets refunded after checkout.

Folio check

Every tax-exempt arrival, before keys

  • Guest status is Tax Exempt.
  • Document is on the guest, readable.
  • Folio shows Posted and Pending.
  • Colorado Tax $0.00 on room charges.
  • Occupation Tax $0.00 on room charges.
  • Incidentals / F&B still have tax unless the same entity is paying those and they still qualify. Default: F&B is taxed.
  • Note is there. Payment matches the entity.

If you are not sure, collect the tax. Give them an itemized folio. Say: “Colorado makes us collect unless the stay is billed to the exempt entity. You can send this folio to DOR if they say we should have dropped it.” Then page a manager. Do not promise a refund at the desk after they have left unless Jessica or a manager already said yes.

What to say

They qualify

“We can take tax off the room when the stay is billed to the agency and we have the certificate or the government card on file. I am going to put that on your guest profile and check that both Colorado Tax and Occupation Tax are zero before I hand keys.”

Military / teacher / personal government travel

“We do 10% off for military and 7% for teachers and government, with ID, on a direct booking. That is a rate, not a tax exemption. Room tax still applies in Cañon City.”

Personal card, work trip

“If the card is in your name and billed to you, Colorado still wants the tax even if work pays you back. If you have a centrally billed government card, a purchase order, or the agency check, we can take tax off.”

Nonprofit without a Colorado certificate

“We need the Colorado exemption certificate. It starts with 98. An IRS letter is not enough by itself. I can book you with tax on, and we can reverse it when you send the certificate and pay with the organization’s card or check.”
