## Nov 20, 2026 · v2.16.0 · page fingerprint 03217c9b…fd41
**Wholesale Cabinets emails go on their jobs by themselves**
- Order confirmations and shipment emails from Wholesale Cabinets, forwarded from hello@fremontmakers.com, arrive in the **Inbox** at the top of the **Shipping** page.
- When the job is certain (the order # is already on it), the email is filed there by itself and the job's log says so.
- Anything else waits under **Needs a job**, with a suggestion like "Looks like Willow Creek Estates" and **File on …**, or a job picker. **Not for a job** sets it aside; **Put back** brings it back.
- Waiting emails show in the sidebar **Shipping** count and on **Today**.
- Only email from Wholesale Cabinets is read. Card and payment details are never kept.

**Shipping, receiving and damage claims**
- Every job has a **Shipping** tab: drop in or paste the Wholesale Cabinets email, or type the order # yourself. Saving the first order records the factory order.
- **Receiving** works on a phone: the rules at the truck, a photo of the bill of lading, **Received** starts the 5-day inspection window, then check off each color group or line.
- **Report damage or a missing item** any time. It builds one claim per order with photos. **Print the claim packet**, **Email it to Wholesale Cabinets** (you attach and send it), then **Mark submitted** and follow the replacement through to **Close the claim**.
- One status line on the job, the order board and the Shipping tab ("In transit · Old Dominion PRO …", "inspect by Nov 10"). Open claims stop close-out until they're closed.
- The **Shipping** page lists what's arriving, what to inspect and open claims. Four new customer emails cover shipped, at our shop, replacements coming and replacements in.

**Change orders end in Finalize**
- The last step of **New change order** is **Finalize**. The change order then shows **1. Download the invoice** and **2. Get it signed** (**E-sign in the tool** or **Paper or emailed PDF**).
- Who signs (buyer name and email, who countersigns, salesperson) is right there, and mistakes are flagged before you send. **Fix a mistake** pulls back a request that's out so you can send it again.

**Cleaner invoices and change orders**
- They look like the original Fremont Makers change order: the logo centered, a large title, details in two columns, totals at the right ending in **Amount due this invoice**.
- Cabinet lines are grouped by color, and each shows the SKU and what it is ("Wall cabinet 30″ wide, 42″ tall").

**Cabinet colors**
- **+ Design** and the Elevations sketch pick a color from chips: the 88 colors on fremontmakers.com/catalog. **+ Add a color** searches the catalog or makes your own, and it goes on the list for everyone.

**What to do:** reload the Project Builder and close old tabs. Your existing jobs, payments and signed copies are unchanged.

## Nov 19, 2026 · v2.15.0 · page fingerprint 3be360e7…d18b
**Starting something new**
- The top of the menu has **Start: + Project** and **+ Design**. **+ Design** opens a new design, ready to draw.

**Getting around**
- Click anywhere on a project's row to open it (and on rows in Customers, Pipeline, Job balances and Job P&L). No more **Open** button.
- The search box just says **Search**. Press / to jump to it.

**The design on the Overview**
- Projects with drawings show **The design** on the **Overview**: a 3D view of the room, the other rooms' floor plans, the finish, and whether the customer approved it. **Open the drawing** takes you to Elevations.

**Emails to the customer**
- The **Overview** has **Email the customer**. It suggests the email that fits where the job is (contract ready to sign, signed, invoices, payment received, cabinets ordered, delivered, thank you and more), fills in names, amounts and dates, and opens it in your mail app or copies it.
- **Company › Email templates** has the wording for all ten. Anyone with Full access can change it; **Go back to the original wording** undoes it.
- Signing emails and **Send to customer** on Invoices use the same wording.

**Change orders and the order board**
- **New change order** opens at the top of the Change orders tab, not below the ones already there.
- On the **Order board**, a job that can't move yet says why (like a balance still open) with a button to fix it, such as **Record the final payment**, which opens that invoice ready to record.

## Nov 19, 2026 · v2.14.0 · page fingerprint 00d9d980…2bec
**Order board**
- **Projects › Order board** shows every signed job by where it is: Contract signed, Deposit paid, Factory ordered, Delivered, Final payment, Complete.
- Drag a card to the next column, or click **Move to…**. It runs the same checks as the job page (no factory order before the deposit, no final payment with a balance open) and logs every move.

**What we actually paid**
- On the **Invoices** tab, **Upload supplier invoice** reads the supplier's PDF. Check what each line was for, then **Add costs**. Or **Add a cost by hand**.
- You'll see the quoted cost, what you actually paid, the margin, and how far over or under the estimate the job came in. **Job P&L** uses what you actually paid once it's logged.

**Closing out a job**
- Once a job is delivered, the **Overview** has a **Close out** card: a final billing check, then **Close out the project**.
- **Open the handoff packet** to print for the customer: what was installed, their warranty and when it runs through, how to care for the cabinets, how to reach us, and a code to leave a review. Save your review link on the card first.
- **Log the review** and **Add a referral**. **Start a project** from a referral fills in their name.

**Sales tax**
- On the **Estimate** tab, step 2: **Use the site address rate** looks up the rate with its state, county and city parts, or **Type a different rate…** with a reason (like a tax-exempt buyer).
- **What's taxed and what isn't** lists it line by line. Tax is on product only; assembly labor isn't taxed.
- Signed jobs keep the tax they were signed with.

## Nov 19, 2026 · v2.13.0 · page fingerprint 4f0b6471…a201
**Start a project your way**
- **New project** asks how to start: **the supplier's SKUs**, **Sketch first**, **From a design**, or **From a previous order**. A sketch or design skips pricing: draw it, get the customer's OK, then price it on the **Estimate** tab.
- **Projects › Designs** keeps cabinet layouts to start projects from. **Start a project from this design** copies it in.
- The customer approves the drawings in person, by email, or with an e-sign link (**Elevations** tab).

**Estimate, then contract**
- The **Estimate** tab goes step by step: Cabinets, Assembly and delivery, Extras and discounts, Review and approve. Review shows every line's margin; **Approve this estimate** before the contract.
- The **Contract** tab is now a builder: steps on the left, the agreement on the right as you type, anything missing in red, special conditions, and plans or photos attached as Exhibit A.

**Signing and invoices**
- The customer sees a short welcome, initials four highlighted sections, then signs. The signed copy shows the initials and both signatures.
- When the customer signs, whoever countersigns gets an email, and **Today** shows **Countersign**.
- Fully signed contracts issue their invoices (numbered like FM-2611-101-1, with the tax split). **Send to customer** and **Send to finance** (to Sara Thorp) walk you through it.

**Change orders**
- **Add additional products**: cabinets, hardware, anything; nothing on the order has to change.
- Builder orders: **The whole order** or **Every house**, as well as one house.
- **Upload SKUs** reads the Wholesale Cabinets PDF, even a scanned one, and adds every item; shipping fees go under Other charges.

**Every day**
- Each project shows five steps (Start · Design · Estimate & contract · Order & deliver · Close out) and **Next up**.
- **Today** on the Dashboard lists what to do. **Search** at the top of the menu (press **/**).
- A **Salesperson** on every project. Changes are never lost: a red **Not saved yet** shows if a save hasn't gone through.
- Elevations: drag an upper cabinet up or down. Invoices: **Add check #** where one's missing.

## Nov 9, 2026 · v2.11.1 · page fingerprint 4593832c…a22f
**The customer's signing page**
- A customer who opens their signing link sees only the document, the signing boxes and then a confirmation: "Signed. Thank you." It has no menu and no way into the Project Builder.
- Once everyone has signed, the customer's page shows **View or save your signed copy**.

**Change orders: add many cabinets at once**
- On the **Cabinets** step, next to **+ Add a cabinet**, there's **Upload SKUs** (the supplier's PDF or a spreadsheet) and **Paste SKUs** (one per line, like "W3042-SBW 2").
- SKUs already on the order or a supplier PDF come in with their price. Any others are listed under **Needs a unit cost**, with **Add with a cost** next to each.
- An uploaded supplier PDF is filed with that change order under **Documents**.

**Your data is safe in updates**
- Updates change the tool, never your jobs, payments, change orders, files or signatures.

## Nov 6, 2026 · v2.11.0 · page fingerprint 1532fc90…6704
**Signing contracts and change orders**
- **Send for signature** on the Contract tab gives the buyer their own link. Send it with **Email it from your mail app**, or **Copy link** to text it. The buyer reads, agrees and signs, then the Fremont Makers signer **countersigns**.
- The signed copy gets a **certificate page**: who signed, when, and from where. It's filed under **Documents**, and the job becomes Contracted.
- Reminders show on day 3 and day 7. Links stop working after 30 days.
- Signing somewhere else? Choose **Sign outside the tool**: save the PDF, send it your usual way, then upload the signed copy and press **Mark signed**.
- Change orders that charge or credit the buyer are sent for signature when you save them.

**Documents tab**
- Four sections: contract and invoices, change orders, supplier orders, and **supporting documents**.
- **Upload** plans, photos, specs and emails to any job. **Remove** hides a file but keeps it, and it can be restored.

**Change orders for every kind of change**
- Start from what happened: **Add**, **Swap**, **Remove / return**, **Backordered**, **Damaged**, **Our mistake** or **Other**.
- Add restocking fees, freight or credits, and set a new delivery date. Record supplier credits, withdraw a change order, and refund a credit.
