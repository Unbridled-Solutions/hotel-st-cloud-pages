## Nov 9, 2026 · v2.11.1 · page fingerprint 4593832c…a22f
**The customer's signing page**
- A customer who opens their signing link sees only the document, the signing boxes and then a confirmation: "Signed. Thank you." It has no menu and no way into the Project Builder.
- Once everyone has signed, the customer's page shows **View or save your signed copy**.

**Change orders: add many cabinets at once**
- On the **Cabinets** step, next to **+ Add a cabinet**, there's **Upload SKUs** (the supplier's PDF or a spreadsheet) and **Paste SKUs** (one per line, like "W3042-SBW 2").
- SKUs already on the order or a supplier PDF come in with their price. Any others are listed under **Needs a unit cost**, with **Add with a cost** next to each.
- An uploaded supplier PDF is filed with that change order under **Documents**.

**Your data is safe in updates**
- Updates change the tool, never your jobs, payments, change orders, files or signatures.

## Nov 6, 2026 · v2.11.0 · page fingerprint 1532fc90…6704
**Signing contracts and change orders**
- **Send for signature** on the Contract tab gives the buyer their own link. Send it with **Email it from your mail app**, or **Copy link** to text it. The buyer reads, agrees and signs, then the Fremont Makers signer **countersigns**.
- The signed copy gets a **certificate page**: who signed, when, and from where. It's filed under **Documents**, and the job becomes Contracted.
- Reminders show on day 3 and day 7. Links stop working after 30 days.
- Signing somewhere else? Choose **Sign outside the tool**: save the PDF, send it your usual way, then upload the signed copy and press **Mark signed**.
- Change orders that charge or credit the buyer are sent for signature when you save them.

**Documents tab**
- Four sections: contract and invoices, change orders, supplier orders, and **supporting documents**.
- **Upload** plans, photos, specs and emails to any job. **Remove** hides a file but keeps it, and it can be restored.

**Change orders for every kind of change**
- Start from what happened: **Add**, **Swap**, **Remove / return**, **Backordered**, **Damaged**, **Our mistake** or **Other**.
- Add restocking fees, freight or credits, and set a new delivery date. Record supplier credits, withdraw a change order, and refund a credit.
