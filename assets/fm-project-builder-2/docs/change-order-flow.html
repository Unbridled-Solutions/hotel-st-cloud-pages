Change orders

How a change order is made today, based on the 8:57 PM recording, and a proposed replacement: one panel inside the job, four steps, priced with the job's own settings, and its own invoice. The prototype below runs on the same change order code that's in the package (src/fm-phase2-changeorder.js).

Today

What happened in the recording

  1. A whole-order PDF went into one house"Willow Creek Change order 10/23/26" has Bayview White, Bayview Wood and Moss Green cabinets for several houses. All 14 lines were added to Cimarron Peak 2 (Bayview White).
  2. The house was switched halfway, and the numbers didn't followThe change was moved to Mesa Ridge 1 (Moss Green) on the price step, but it kept Cimarron Peak 2's cabinets and totals ("FM-2609-781 stays $7,153.81").
  3. "Add" lowered a quantityAdding the PDF to the existing list changed SM8(SCR) from 3 to 1 instead of adding to it.
  4. The new lines ignored the job's markupExisting lines were at 72%; the added lines came in at the 35% "All markup" default.
  5. "Courtesy $0" was selected by defaultFor a $7,466.38 change. One Next click would have given it away.
  6. A typed amount skipped tax and G&A$7,800 was typed in; the review showed "bulk 0% · G&A 0% · tax 0%" and no reason beyond "skus updated".
  7. Billing defaulted to folding into the schedule"Keep in payment schedule" was preselected. You've decided each change order gets its own invoice.
  8. It's a pop-up off All ProjectsStarted from the list, with no buyer email filled in, and an earlier change order is still sitting in All Projects as its own "project" (FM-2610-475, $8,740.71).

Proposed

One panel in the job, four steps

1What and whyStarted from the job, so the house can't drift. A whole-order PDF is split by color and assigned to houses. Pick a reason.
2CabinetsDrop the supplier PDF (same import as New Project). Adding never lowers a quantity. Wrong-color cabinets are flagged.
3PricePriced with the job's own markup, discount, G&A and tax. Charge it, courtesy, or an agreed amount with a reason. Nothing preselected.
4Invoice and sendIts own invoice, due on signing. Customer page preview, then Dropbox Sign or paper. The job total moves when it's signed.
