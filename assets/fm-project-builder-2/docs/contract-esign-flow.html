Contract and e-sign

How a quote becomes a signed contract today, based on the 1:47 PM recording, and a proposed replacement in which Dropbox Sign (HelloSign) opens in a frame inside the job. The prototype at the bottom runs on the same contract code that's in the package (src/fm-phase2-contract.js). Dropbox Sign is simulated.

Today

What happened in the recording

  1. A $0 contract was issued"Next: Issue Contract" locked an empty quote: no customer, no signer, no project, no cabinets. The packet printed "Contract total $0.00" on every page.
  2. The lock code is printed in the dialogThe instructions show the 4-digit password in plain text, so it protects nothing. Anyone who opens the dialog can read it.
  3. Dropbox Sign failed after the job was lockedThe request was refused ("upgrade to a paid API plan or use test_mode") in a browser alert. The job was already Contracted, nothing was sent, and nothing recorded that.
  4. The deposit schedule counts back from installDeposits are set 90 and 45 days before install. With install typed as Nov 2028, the "50% today" deposit moved to Aug 2028. With an install two weeks out, the deposit would land in the past.
  5. The packet opens in a separate tabIt has print-only signature lines and no e-sign fields. The status changes to Contracted before anyone signs.
  6. Buyer email is typed twiceOnce in the job header, once in the dialog. The signer name isn't sent to Dropbox Sign at all, and Fremont Makers never countersigns.
  7. The summary lags behindRight after locking, Project Summary still said "proposed · $0.00" until the page was reloaded.

Proposed

Check, review, send, track: in one Contract tab on the job

1Ready checkCustomer, signer, email, address, cabinets, a total above $0, clean quote math and a sensible schedule. Blockers link to the step that fixes them.
2ReviewPacket preview in the page, with the schedule in dollars. Deposit due on signing, last payment at install.
3SendEmail it, check it in a Dropbox Sign frame first, or sign in person in the frame. Pricing freezes while it's out.
4TrackOpened, signed by the buyer, then countersigned by Fremont Makers. The job becomes Contracted when everyone has signed, in Dropbox Sign or on paper (upload the wet-signed copy). The signed copy is attached.

Job status

Proposed→ Ready to contract→ Out for signature→ Buyer signed→ Contracted

Contracted only happens when everyone has signed: all signatures in Dropbox Sign, or a wet-signed copy uploaded (owner decision, Oct 27). Declined or canceled goes back to Proposed with the quote unlocked. If Dropbox Sign refuses a request, the job stays Ready to contract and the error is shown in the page and logged.
