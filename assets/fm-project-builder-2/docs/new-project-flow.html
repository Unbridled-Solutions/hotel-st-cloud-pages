New Project flow

What creating a project looks like today, based on the 11:20 AM recording, and a proposed replacement. The prototype at the bottom works: it prices the Braddy Penrose job live with the same quote code that's in the Phase 1 package.

Today

Two paths, both in stacked pop-ups

Builder order
  1. Single or builder?
  2. →
  3. Which builder?
  4. →
  5. How many homes per model
  6. →
  7. Address + color per house
  8. →
  9. Create order: houses have no cabinets yet

Then each house needs its own supplier PDF import and pricing, from the All Projects list. Willow Creek meant 10 imports for 5 models.

Single house
  1. Single or builder?
  2. →
  3. Upload PDF or blank
  4. →
  5. Find the file in Finder
  6. →
  7. Markup per line (35% everywhere)
  8. →
  9. Create quote: no customer, no total seen

Customer, signer, email, address and dates are typed afterwards on the quote screen.

  1. Cancel throws everything awayFour pop-ups deep there's no Back button and no draft. In the recording, both paths ended with Cancel and nothing was kept.
  2. Single house never asks who it's forThe quote is created without a customer, so it sits in All Projects as "—" until someone fills in the header fields.
  3. No price before "Create"The markup step shows line sell only. No discount, assembly, G&A, tax or total, so you commit to a quote you haven't seen priced.
  4. 35% default fits no real jobWillow Creek ran 18–72% by model, Braddy 30%. Items that go out at cost (TU, PWB18K, TUK, F342) have to be zeroed by hand every time.
  5. Identical houses priced one by oneHouses of the same model are identical line for line, but a builder order still needs one PDF import per house.
  6. Color is picked by handThe SKU already says it: SBW = Bayview White, UMO = Moss Green, SWD = Bayview Wood, VDH = Dark Hickory. A hand-picked color can disagree with the cabinets.
  7. Pricing settings are hiddenDiscount, G&A, tax and assembly change from job to job (Penrose was 5% / 5% / 8.7%, Willow Creek 12% / 2% / 7.9%), but the flow never shows them. Only the payment plan appears, as grey text tied to the builder.
  8. No review, no draft, no recordThe job code is assigned silently, nothing confirms what was created, and nothing records who created it.
Proposed

One page, five steps, the price always visible

A full page instead of pop-ups. It autosaves as a Draft from the first keystroke, so you can leave and come back. Single homes and builder orders use the same steps; "how many homes" is just a number.

1CustomerSearch or add. Contact, signer and email only. No pricing is carried over from the customer.Fixes 2
2HomesOne home, or models × quantity for a builder order.Fixes 5
3CabinetsDrop the supplier PDF once per model. Lines, color and totals are checked.Fixes 5, 6
4PriceSet this job's markup, discount, G&A, tax, assembly and payment plan. Starts from company defaults, or copy a past job. Live total and margin.Fixes 3, 4, 7
5ReviewHomes, addresses, payment plan in dollars. Create, which is logged by name.Fixes 1, 8
Prototype · interactive

Try it with Braddy Penrose

Real lines and costs from the Penrose contract and recording. On the Price step, set this job's markup, discount, G&A, tax, assembly and payment plan. They start from company defaults; "Copy from a past job" fills in Penrose's signed settings. You can also edit single lines or switch on "priced by bid". The summary on the right recalculates with the Phase 1 quote math.

Prototype only: nothing is saved. Addresses, the builder-order model list and the company defaults are examples. Penrose lines, costs and its signed settings (in "Copy from a past job") are real.
