Corner Suites
Fixed Corridor Constraint. The existing corridor is locked in place — position, width (~8'), and both face walls are immovable historic structure. This fixes the depth of every room on both sides of the building. North rooms are fixed at ~20' deep (corridor face to north exterior wall). South rooms are fixed at ~27' deep (corridor face to south exterior wall). The only design lever is the interior partition walls between cells, which can be removed or relocated to create wider rooms that compensate for fixed depth.
Cell-Combining Strategy. Original monastic cells are ~11'-10" to ~14'-2" wide. At the fixed north depth (~20'), a single 12'-wide cell yields only ~240 sq ft — just under the 250 sq ft minimum. The solution: combine adjacent narrow cells into single guest rooms ~17–20' wide. This reduces the count from ~14 original cells per side to 8 guest rooms per side, comfortably clearing 250 sq ft on north and 340+ sq ft on south. No structural or exterior walls are touched. Only interior partitions between original cells are opened up.

Design + Structural Notes
Fixed Constraints vs. Flexible Elements

🔒 Fixed — Cannot Change

  • Corridor: position, width (~8'), both face walls — all immovable
  • North exterior wall and window openings
  • South exterior wall and window openings
  • East and west end walls
  • All room depths: both sides fixed by corridor position
  • Floor-to-floor height (important for MEP routing)

✏️ Flexible — Can Be Modified

  • Interior partition walls between original cells — can be removed or repositioned
  • New bathroom locations (anywhere interior, with new plumbing rough-in)
  • Closet / wardrobe sizing and placement within room envelope
  • New corridor doors (new openings through corridor face walls — structural review required)
  • Window treatments, sills, glazing system (within historic opening)

📐 Cell-Combining Math

  • Original cells: ~12' wide (11'-10") and ~14' wide (14'-2")
  • North rooms at ~20' deep: need ~14' wide minimum to hit 250 sq ft → wider cells can stand alone; narrow 12' cells must combine
  • South rooms at ~27' deep: even a 10' cell = 270 sq ft → most can stand alone, but combining gives premium sizing
  • Recommended: standardize all rooms at ~20' wide by combining 1–2 original cells per room
  • This yields 8 rooms per side = 16 total, consistent room sizing, simpler FF&E procurement

🚿 Bathroom Placement

  • Bathrooms should stack vertically between floors — locate on same wall each level to share a plumbing chase
  • Keep baths away from window walls — don't sacrifice the view with a bathroom
  • North rooms: bath on corridor side or east/west partition (south-inner corner)
  • South rooms: bath on corridor side or east/west partition (north-inner corner)
  • Wet walls back-to-back between adjacent rooms reduces pipe runs

🏛️ Cloister Arcade — Outdoor Colonnade

  • The cloister arcade is an outdoor covered walkway attached to the north face — not an enclosed interior space
  • North room windows look through the arcade columns to the grounds beyond
  • Opportunity: restore the arcade as a signature guest amenity — morning coffee, reading, contemplative walk
  • Plant climbing vines on columns, stone pavers, simple bench seating
  • Can be glassed in seasonally (retractable system) without altering the structure

📋 Next Steps

  • Commission measured as-built drawings — confirm exact room depths and cell widths
  • Structural assessment: which interior partition walls are load-bearing vs. infill
  • Engage SHPO — confirm historic tax credit eligibility before any design work
  • MEP preliminary: plumbing stack locations, HVAC strategy (individual fan coils recommended)
  • Confirm total number of existing cells per floor — affects final room count and width math
  • ADA compliance plan: minimum 2 accessible rooms per level, elevator if multi-story
