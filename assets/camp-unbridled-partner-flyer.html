This is company camp, not a conference. We asked a small table of partners because you helped write the last 25 years. Come eat with us, ride the water, walk the 5K, and help us celebrate the people who made Unbridled possible.

How the weekend feels

Wednesday · Sept 30

Welcome to the Rockies

Arrive by train, shuttle, or car. Sunglasses from Zeal. Then dinner on the Event Lawn. First night is about belonging, not a program.

Thursday morning · Oct 1

Unbridled Regatta

Twelve miles on Grand Lake. Stories, peaks, and a Sagebrush BBQ in town. Toast and group photo at Grand Lake Lodge on the way home.

Thursday afternoon · Oct 1

Golf cart parade

Teams turn carts into 25-year floats. Inside jokes, handmade signs, a 90-second moment at the judges. Partners can ride or judge.

Then Thursday night

The neighborhood takes over

Block party at Jericho Junction. Dinner in the street. No presentations. Flip the page for Friday: Steps for Good, the games, and 2001.

Thursday evening · Oct 1

Block Party at Jericho Junction

Dinner in the street between cabins. No presentations. If fire restrictions hold, we use propane. The night still works. This is the conversation you cannot have in a ballroom.

Friday night · Oct 2

2001 Time Machine

We were born in 2001, so Friday night is the anniversary party. Games medals get announced. Then we dance. Come dressed like you remember the year.

Friday morning · UnbridledACTS

Steps for Good

A 5K you can run, jog, or walk. The race is the experience. The impact is Grand County students: shoes, socks, shirts, books, a meal, and quiet support through the school for kids who cannot be there. $40,000, protected for the community first.

How we want you there

Be a guest of the house

Sit at dinner. Ride a boat. Walk the 5K or hand medals. Do not work the weekend like a booth.

Be named, not loud

We thank partners from the grounds and at the moments you host. Camp is not a sponsor reel.

Leave something real

A judge’s chair. A story at the block party. A pair of shoes on Friday. People, not a logo wall.
