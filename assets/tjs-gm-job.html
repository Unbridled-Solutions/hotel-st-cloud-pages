About TJ's

TJ's Log & Timber builds and mills log homes in Bailey, Colorado. We are part of Unbridled Companies, a family office whose mission is Doing Good by Doing Well. Our mill crew is the pride of the place. Customers still have to feel that from the lot, the lobby, and the first conversation.

This seat is the operating lead on the ground. You are not a remote brand manager. You are in the yard, in the mill, and in the room with the buyer. You protect skilled jobs. You keep promises on kits and stacks. You make the customer face match the quality of the wood.

About Bailey

This role lives at 62160 US Highway 285, Bailey, Colorado. On-site. The mill is here. The walk-ins are here. The roadside is here. If mountain-town life on 285 can be home, we want to talk.

Bailey sits in Platte Canyon, about an hour from Denver on the way toward Fairplay and South Park. Real mill town. Real weather. People stop because they saw a cabin from the road. You will be here for that.

Position summary

You will run day-to-day operations for TJ's: mill, yard, sales floor, and the jobs we have promised to stack. You will set the hospitality standard for every person who walks in. You will keep the mill busy without burning the crew. You will tell the founder the truth about schedule, cash, and what a customer actually experienced.

What you will lead

  • Daily mill and yard operations: safety, flow, and a clean, ready shop
  • The customer face: arrival, lobby, conference room, bathrooms, hours, and how we treat a walk-in
  • Sales from first hello through a sold kit: models, quotes, deposits, and honest lead times
  • Production against promise: mill-to-square, kit, and stack dates that we can keep
  • People: hiring, coaching, and protecting skilled mill jobs; morale is part of the work
  • Roadside and display: 285 presence, model cabins, Open and Closed signs, walk-ins welcome
  • A simple weekly rhythm the investors can trust: jobs, cash, crew, and what is slipping

What we are looking for

  • You have run a shop, mill, plant, or construction yard, or you have been a home builder or a project manager for a large construction company. Logs help. A real operating floor matters more than a log-home brand on the resume.
  • You understand the full life cycle of a build, from design through finish: Gantt charts, budget, permitting, inspections, and certificate of occupancy. If you have run a whole construction project that way, the log is just a different material.
  • You can sell without being slick. Five to ten walk-ins a day is a hospitality job as much as a production job.
  • You keep dates. Kits and stacks have names on them. You would rather tell the truth on a date than miss it.
  • You are present. This is Bailey, on-site, not a Denver commute with a laptop.
  • You raise the standard without making people small. Character, change, credibility, community.

What makes this role unique

The mill already works. You are not being hired to invent log homes. You are being hired so the company feels alive at the front door, honest on the schedule, and proud of the people who cut the wood. Unbridled's 20/20/60 model still applies: do well so we can do good. This seat is how TJ's earns that.

Compensation and benefits

Compensation and benefits are negotiable and will match the person's experience. Benefits today include health, dental, vision, and company-paid life, short-term, and long-term disability.

This is a full-time, on-site role. Mill weeks and stack weeks will ask more of you. We will be honest about that, and we will try to take care of the person doing the work.
