The work is real. The storefront is 2015.

A SWOT of Unbridled Media as a company and as a website. Honest, not a cheer sheet. Written so we can decide what to sell, what to fix, and what not to pretend.

Unbridled Media, LLC · live events family (Unbridled Solutions) · 8 Sep 2026. Sources: unbridledmedia.com (live this morning), Clutch profile 962207, vault directory, Capture Co operating agreement, Commander Round Table notes. Not a valuation. Not a headcount census.

The shop Strong mid-market. About a 7 / 10.

Real partners, real clients, real event-video muscle next to Productions. Purpose is not a slide. That is the company.

The website A 4 / 10 storefront on a 7 / 10 product.

WordPress from 2015. Broken nav paths. Blog last posted early 2024. SWAG says Unauthorized. A buyer who judges the shop by the site will under-rank the work.

Do not rank this shop against Vice, Anonymous Content, or a holding-company video mill on size. Rank it against independent branded-content and event-video houses in St. Louis, Denver, and the Unbridled live-events lane. That is the sport they actually play.

Strengths

S

  • Partners who have actually made the work. Ben Lamb, Mike Walsh, Joe Seebeck. Event video, branded content, commercial. Not a freelancer directory with a logo on it.
  • Client proof on the homepage: WashU, Maritz, Audacy, Graybar, WD-40, AssetMark, Luxco, plus logos for Edward Jones, Marriott, Bayer, Chipotle, Smithsonian, Lexus. That is a book, not a mood board.
  • Two real offices on Unbridled real estate: 1115 Grant, Denver, and 624 Trade Center, Chesterfield. Same family of buildings as the rest of the house.
  • Sits inside Unbridled Solutions with Productions, Travel, Capture Co, GIFT'D, and OrangeDoor. Capture Co operating agreement: Unbridled Media, LLC holds 60%. Event days already need cameras. That is a built-in pipeline if they use it.
  • Purpose is written in public: 20% of proceeds back. Internships on real sets. That matches Doing Good by Doing Well, even if the ACTS vs scholarship wording on the site is not fully aligned (see Weaknesses).

W

  • The website is the loudest weakness. HOME / DEMO REEL / WHAT WE DO / OUR WORK / ABOUT are mostly one long homepage. Typing /our-work/ and /about/ 404s. A procurement person who bookmarks "Our Work" gets a dead page.
  • Stories have not moved since early 2024 (Derik, Kelly, Ben, Anabelle). Headshots post is September 2023. A shop that lives on new work is publishing like it stopped two years ago.
  • SWAG page title is "Our Swag" and the body is "Unauthorized." Twitter is still in the nav in 2026. Orange 2015 icons. It reads like a theme that never got a second designer pass. Stan said the quiet part this week: the design chops are not the work.
  • Clutch is unclaimed. Lists 2 to 9 employees, Kirkwood, founded 2015, no reviews, no case studies. The live site shows a much larger named bench. Buyers who start on Clutch will think this is a tiny unproven shop.
  • Giveback copy does not match itself. Homepage: scholarships at Mizzou and Webster, plus Camping & Education Foundation. Clutch: 20% to UnbridledACTS group homes. Ecosystem DNA is 20/20/60 via UnbridledACTS. Until that sentence is one sentence, purpose looks sloppy to a careful buyer.

O

  • OrangeDoor plus Unbridled is a transatlantic live-events story that still needs cameras. Media should be on that invoice, not a third-party London crew, unless the client already has one.
  • In-house content for Hotel St. Cloud, Fremont, Standard Oil, TJ's, GIFT'D. The ecosystem already spends on story. Media should be the default, not a vendor bake-off.
  • Commander Round Table is Media hosting, Productions shadowing. That is the right room to show AI as a crew, not a chatbot, if the work on screen is theirs.
  • Mike already has a live agent (Media Mike, Aug 19). A rebuilt site plus a real intake (reel, three case studies, one quote path) would let that agent sell while the partners shoot.

T

  • AI video is eating the "we can turn a recap around fast" middle. If the pitch is speed and competence, a thousand shops now say the same thing with a model. The pitch has to be character, event craft, and Unbridled as one operating system.
  • Holding-company agencies will unbundle video from the show. If Productions and Media do not look like one house on the website, procurement will split them.
  • A buyer lands on unbridledmedia.com, hits a 404 or Unauthorized, and hires the shop with the cleaner reel site. The work never gets in the room.
  • Partner concentration. Ben, Mike, Joe. Same pattern as OrangeDoor: the relationship is the deal. If the site still reads as a 2015 blog, the next generation of buyers never meets them.

What I would do

  1. Do not rebuild the brand before you rebuild the paths. Make Our Work and About real URLs. Kill SWAG until it is finished. Take Twitter out of the chrome.
  2. One sentence on the 20%. UnbridledACTS, scholarships, or both. Right now the homepage and Clutch tell two stories.
  3. Three case studies with a problem, a film, and what happened. AssetMark, WashU, one Unbridled live event. That is the sales deck. The reel is not enough.
  4. Sell the family. Productions plus Media plus OrangeDoor as one content-and-show offer. The website still sells "a video shop in two cities." That understates the machine.
  5. Claim or delete Clutch. An unclaimed 2-to-9-person Kirkwood listing is a tax on every inbound that starts there.
