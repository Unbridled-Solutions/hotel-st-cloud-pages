What Stan puts on the screen

House rules

  • No status. If it could have been Slack, it does not get the table.
  • Show the work. A prompt, a draft, a miss. Theory without an artifact waits.
  • Character first. If we would not frame it, it does not leave the building.
  • One experiment each. Ambition is not a substitute for a named try.
  • Productions shadows. Questions yes. Pitching a stack, no.

Come with · skip

  • Curiosity. New people: “none yet” is an honest win.
  • One sentence roadblock if you have one.
  • Skip a tour of tools you have not used.
  • Skip fear that you are behind. Empathy, then a sticky.
  • Skip the personality quiz today. That is Friday.

What good looks like at 90

  • Names on the door. Wins and roadblocks already typed.
  • Everyone can repeat: chief of staff, specialists, you are the gate.
  • One stealable artifact, even if it was Stan’s pictures.
  • One roadblock with a decision or a named experiment.
  • Every Media person has a Friday proof. Next week’s owner is named.
