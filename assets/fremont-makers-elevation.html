MC Suggestions
3-drawer banks on both ends are a great call for an office galley — silverware, utensils, and office snacks right where you need them, no bending to dig through base cabinets.
With 10ft ceilings, 42" uppers leave 24" to ceiling. The 4" crown moulding bridges most of that gap — consider a full-height soffit above crown to fully close off the space for a cleaner look.
Trash pull-out at 12" fits a standard single-bin pull-out unit. Placing it next to the drawer bank (and before the DW) keeps the trash close to the prep side without being right next to the sink.
Granite countertop on a 12ft run will require two slabs with one seam. Best seam placement: 72" from left (between DW and sink base) — falls at a natural cabinet joint and stays functional.
The sink at center and DW to its right is the ideal ergonomic workflow: scrape trash → rinse at sink → load the dishwasher — all in one natural left-to-right motion. Smart.
