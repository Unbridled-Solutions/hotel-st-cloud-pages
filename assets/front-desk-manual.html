Check-in

Before they walk up

  • Every arrival room must be inspected by front desk. Note it on the reservation. No note = you inspect before keys go out.
  • Express check-in = green check on Arrivals. Confirm ID in Documents and a card on file.
  • Dirty rooms can still show “available.” Walk it or check Flexkeeping.

“Perfect. I’ve found your reservation, Mr/Mrs ___. We have you in one of our [room type], checking out [date].”
  1. ID + the card they want on file.

Also useful: TV power is the button on the bottom of the set, then the remote. Polaroid is free — favorite photos go in the jar with last name + phone. Extra outlets are behind the nightstand. Vanity drawers that don’t pull easily are intentional (old plumbing).

Room inspection checklist

Swiffer, arrival list, phone. Same-day bookings: inspect before they walk in.

  • Lights on · TV on (wait until the picture stays)
  • Dust: headboard, sills, vanity, frames, mirrors
  • Sealed water in closet · Bible + night light in nightstand · pen/notepad · amenity card · DND on inside of door
  • Two robes · fridge waters (2, or 4 in double queens) · photo booklet on dresser
  • Safe unlocked: ## then six zeros
  • Coffee caddy stocked · towels complete · clock correct
  • Windows closed and actually latched (bugs)
  • Mini-split 72°. Summer: cool, bath heater off. Winter: heat, bath floor heater on 6
  • Note the inspection on the reservation

Early check-in

Standard 3pm. 12pm early is usually $30. If they arrive unannounced and want it faster, still $30. If the room is already clean, complimentary is usually fine.

Folio → ADD/ADJUST CHARGE → Add Item → Room Upgrades → Early Check-In. Confirm the right card. Don’t use the blue Check In on a multi-room reservation unless the whole group is arriving.

Check-out

Guest leaving

  • How was the room and the stay? Offer luggage help. Ask for a Google review if it went well.
  • Thank them for making St. Cloud part of their story. Safe travels.
  • Balance $0 → mark CHECKED OUT. If not, Folio → Add Payment. Split amounts if they want multiple cards.
  • Never check out with a remaining balance. Decline → grab a manager.

Late check-out

11am standard. 1pm is $30, or complimentary if occupancy allows (ask 24 hours ahead when you can). Folio → Room Upgrades → Late Check-Out. If they add it after arrival, keys die at 11am until you recode.

Invoice / confirmation

ACTIONS → Email Folio. Confirmation: ACTIONS → Compose Message → Custom → Reservation Confirmed. BCC yourself so you know it sent.

Groups & allotments

Lorin owns most groups. Caller wants to plan an event: name, phone, email, dates, what they want → pass to events. Caller is in a group: get the group name, read the group sheet, book only if the notes say you can. If it says wait for Lorin, take a message.

Rates, tax exempt, moving rooms

Tax exempt: get the document, attach to the guest (not the reservation), set status Tax Exempt. If they already paid, Folio → adjust Colorado Tax and Occupation Tax separately.

Military / government / other plans: after search, Display → check all boxes → pick the discounted rate. Copy docs for military/gov. Any other discount: ask a manager first.

Move a room: Calendar, drag to the new room. Same type/dates = no override. Different type or dates = confirm with the guest. If they requested a room, assign it and note it.

Main Street view: 201–203, 301–303, 401–403. ADA: 202 and 402 (302 is backup, not fully ADA). Best mountain views: 4th floor.

Cancel, no-show, refund

Cancellation: within 7 days we keep first night; within 2 days we keep the full stay. Ask why, note it, set Confirmed → Cancelled, send confirmation, refund if they’re entitled.

No-call no-show: 10:30pm Whistle (are you still coming? we release at 1am). 2am Whistle (cancelled / charged). Note the profile. Tell a manager. Don’t cancel yourself unless you’re sure the card will take the stay.

Refund: Folio gear → Refund Transaction, then adjust Room Rate (pre-tax) so Balance Due zeros. Split folio: ADD FOLIO → move selected transactions.

Cloudbeds Error 500 (keys)

Encoder isn’t talking to Cloudbeds. Windows → search Cloudbeds Integrator. If you don’t see the Ambiance logo, toggle Running off then on. If the logo is already there, or it still fails — manager.

Guest issues

Noise

  1. Log time, complaining room, nature of noise.
  2. Apologize, say you’ll handle it now.
  3. First contact: call or visit. Quiet hours 10pm–7am.
“Good evening, this is the front desk. We’ve received a noise complaint from a neighboring guest. We kindly ask you to lower the volume. Thank you.”

Second complaint: in person with a supervisor if available, formal warning (possible eviction, no refund). Continues: management, full incident report. Follow up with the guest who called. Always file the form.

Incident report

Reported pest in a room

Treat any guest report of insects in a room the same way — bat bugs, bed bugs, or anything else they describe. (Bat bugs look a lot like bed bugs; don’t diagnose at the desk.)

Apologize, don’t downplay. Name, room, date/time, what they saw. Notify MOD and housekeeping supervisor. Offer a non-adjacent room and help with luggage. Mark the room Out of Service. Pest control if confirmed. Follow up.

Hotel story

Why “St. Cloud”

St. Cloud is the patron saint of healing. Colorado drew people seeking recovery from tuberculosis — the name is part of that story.

How the building got here

  • 1878–79 — built in Silver Cliff at the height of the silver boom (Silver Cliff was Colorado’s third-largest city).
  • 1886 — boom over, leased land gone. Owners dismantled it brick by brick and moved it to Cañon City.
  • 1887 — rebuilt and expanded at 631 Main. Two more floors added. Still the address.
  • First hotel in Colorado with electricity and running water in every room. Cañon City’s first working elevator.
  • Last operated as a hotel in 2003 before this restoration.

Hollywood of Colorado

1909–11: Selig Polyscope based here. Silent-film crews stayed with us — Tom Mix, Bill Duncan, Joe Ryan.

1940s–50s: Westerns around the Royal Gorge. Guests included Burt Lancaster, Slim Pickens, Charles Bronson, Broderick Crawford, Hugh O’Brien, Scott Brady, Robert Walker.

Royal Gorge Railroad War, 1878: D&RG vs Santa Fe over the one-track canyon. Sabotage, courts, hired guns — no deaths. The only war in Colorado with none. Photo is in the hallway by the bathrooms.

Original pieces guests can see

  • Newel posts and bannisters: seven layers of paint stripped, then refinished (Jeff Dunn, Penrose Furnishings — also the casings and upstairs rails).
  • Landing light fixtures, stained glass above doors, original transoms.
  • FPH barback arches: original, hand-carved, restored. Top wood piece was made to match.
  • 2nd-floor cowboy queens sit on original structural beams — that’s why the restaurant has no posts. They’re holding up that part of the building.
  • Upstairs: every hallway/room photo is unique (no duplicates). Fake doors sit where the original room entries were.
  • Paintings: Glenn Norberg, local Cañon City artist.

1887 dining room — booths & tables

  • Booth 1 (table 40) — famous figures, including John C. Frémont (Fremont County).
  • Booth 2 (table 41) — food & agriculture: orchards, cattle.
  • Booth 3 (table 42) — film & western heritage.
  • Booth 4 (table 43) — Royal Gorge Bridge & railroad.
  • Table 11 — Royal Gorge Bridge opening day, 1929. Highest suspension bridge in the world then: 955 feet over the Arkansas.
  • Table 12 — Dal DeWeese, explorer, rancher, conservationist.
  • Table 13 — original dining room shortly after the Cañon City reopen, late 1880s.
  • Skyline Drive photo (back table) — built 1905 with prison labor from the Territorial Correctional Facility. Donated, digitized, printed on metal.

War Room & Fremont Public House

War Room is not 1880s — added as a place to step out of the chaos: dream, strategize, talk. Silver buffalo-nickel pins in the cushion buttons. Real books, many first editions. Civil War bullets on display. Premium liquor and a cocktail list.

FPH bar: original restored arches; the top wood was built to match.
