I am interested in paying what I owe. I am just a little confused by the process. We have been running with very little help from Mac and Reliant for probably a year now.

To my recollection, there has not been somebody from Reliant on site since the middle to end of 2024. We have been operating this site without supervision, without anybody from Reliant, for over 18 months now.

I do not want to go back and forth. Take a look from a real-work perspective. Tell me what we could potentially owe you. We will take it from there.

How we got here

I wanted to go back and remember how this relationship started. We began with Justin Walsh in or around early spring 2024. As you know, that did not go well, and we moved him off the project.

He brought in David Mills from Florida. David was equally not up to the task. Then we brought in Lucas Bryan. The commute back and forth to Pueblo caught up with him, and by winter of 2024 he backed out.

We finally landed on Mac, who is amazing. We were supposed to have the project finished sometime in summer 2025. As Mac began to retire, he began to scale back.

Who has been doing the work

I hired Scott Parker as an owner's rep. Scott sourced our trim carpentry, our windows, and our electrical. What was left under Reliant's purview, as I remember it, was Kahar plumbing, and I think Kevin from Comfort Heating out of Hartsel.

Mac has been generous going back and forth, and I am interested in fulfilling the work Mac actually did. I think we were only using Mac an hour a week, maybe two or three. Scott was doing the majority of the work, as was Brian the electrician and Billy the trim carpenter. We also had our own log-chinking contractor that we found.

Halfway through this project we kind of threw the contract out the window. We have been piecing a team together. That is the job as it actually ran.

The ask

I would like you to look at this from a real-work perspective and get back to me on what we could potentially owe you.

Sorry to put this at your feet. I was just really shocked. Thank you.
