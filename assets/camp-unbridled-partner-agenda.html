Open with this

“We did not invite you here to watch a program. We invited you because you helped build the last 25 years, and we want you in the room for the next ones.”

What this isCompany camp for Unbridled live-events people, plus a small partner table. Cabins, not ballrooms. Three nights. One anniversary.
What it is notA sales meeting, a pitch stage, or a client showcase. If it starts feeling like a trade show, we already lost.
Why partnersWest End Entertainment, Zeal Optics, ACCESS DMC, FOH Productions, and the sponsors still coming in. You are guests of the house. Presence beats inventory.
The barIf we would not frame it, it does not ship. Same rule for the weekend: hospitality, not heroics.

The three-day arc

Wed 9/30Arrive. Welcome to the Rockies.

People land by train, shuttle, and car. Check-in, cabin gifts, then the lawn. This night has one job: make 148 teammates and a handful of partners feel like they already belong.

  • 5–7 PM · Zeal Experience at Summit Bar & Grill. Branded sunglasses. Digital redemption ships to anyone who cannot stand in the line.
  • 5–9 PM · Welcome to the Rockies on the Event Lawn. Colorado dinner. First Camp for two OrangeDoor colleagues. A glass with the people who helped get us here.
  • After hours at Summit Bar & Grill. Keep it human. No program on a microphone after dinner.

Say the names: West End Entertainment · Zeal Optics · ACCESS DMC

Thu 10/1Grand Lake. Then the neighborhood.

Breakfast at Headwaters, then out to the water. This is the day partners remember, because it is Colorado and it is not staged in a hotel ballroom.

  • 10 AM–2:45 PM · Unbridled Regatta from Green Ridge Boat Launch. Twelve miles of Grand Lake stories, then Sagebrush BBQ at the pavilion and free time in town.
  • 2:45–3:45 PM · Grand Lake Lodge toast and the group photo. One cocktail. One deck. Get everyone in the frame.
  • Late afternoon · Golf cart parade. Theme: 25 Years of Blazing Our Own Trail. Teams turn carts into floats. Partners can judge or ride; do not sit this one out in a blazer.
  • 6–9 PM · Block Party at Jericho Junction. Dinner in the street. No presentations. If fire restrictions hold, propane only; the night still works.

Say the name: UnbridledACTS on Steps for Good

The giveback, in one minute

“We do not come to a town, take the view, and leave. Twenty percent of how we run this company is generosity. Friday morning is that, in shoes and miles.”

Steps for Good · $40,000 · Grand County students. Working model: bring a school-approved group (aiming 4th and 5th grade) to the onsite 5K. Every student who comes gets shoes, socks, shirt, book, meal, and a medal. Remaining dollars go quietly through the school for kids who cannot be there. Teachers get a real thank-you, not a tote bag speech. Protect the community impact first. Employee shirts come from Camp, not out of the giveback if we can help it.

How you want partners in the room

Be present Ride the train energy or the shuttle. Sit at dinner. Walk the 5K or hand medals. Do not work the room like a booth.
Be named, not loud We thank you from the stage and on the grounds. We do not turn Camp into a sponsor reel. Fulfillment is Carl. Contracting is Sabrina.
Leave something A judge’s seat at the parade. A story at the block party. A pair of shoes on Friday. The memory should be people, not a logo wall.

If they ask the practical questions

Where we sleepSun Outdoors Rocky Mountains. Cabin and tax on the Camp master account Sept 30 to Oct 3. Extra nights are on the guest.
How people get thereAmtrak California Zephyr Denver to Granby (same-cabin block). Westin DIA lounge for fly-in teammates, then shuttles. Drivers and carpools from Denver.
Who is missingA few teammates are on overlapping client programs that same week. They still get a surprise-and-delight from site. We do not leave our people out of a 25-year night just because a show called.
Fire rulesStage 2 was in effect as of June 26. Wood pits and charcoal are out. Propane fire features and gas grills are the plan. Block Party still happens.
SaturdayQuiet departure. Shuttles to Union Station and DEN. No closing general session. Send them home tired and glad.

Close

“Twenty-five years ago this was a hillside and a download. The elk is still the point. Character. People. Doing good by doing well. If you are in this room, you already helped write that story. Come to Granby and help us live it for four days.”

Then stop talking. Ask what they want to host, judge, or fund on Friday. Write the answer down.
