Chief Financial Officer

Cañon City, Colorado · Full-time, on-site · Reports to the Founder

Come build the financial home for a family office that is trying to do good by doing well. You will live in Cañon City, care for the numbers the way we care for people, and help our operating companies feel supported, clear, and ready for what is next.

This seat sits in the family office, not inside a single brand. You will serve hospitality, real estate holdings, newer operating businesses, our wealth company, and UnbridledACTS (our 501(c)(3)) when they need you. Live events companies keep their own finance team.

We are looking for a hands-on CFO. Ten or more years in multi-entity finance, a true expert in Excel, and someone who is both expert and kind.

  • Own the books, the close, and cash across multiple companies
  • Build models other people can trust
  • Partner with brand leaders and our founder
  • Help refresh employee benefits as part of the first-year work
