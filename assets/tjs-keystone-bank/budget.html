What moves the number

Well + septic

If Mary Drive has no tap, well through granite is $40–75k. Engineered septic is in the mid already. Get a neighbor well log before the commitment letter.

Who stacks the logs

Stan mid assumes TJ’s / owner-led stack — no 17% GC. A mountain GC on the same hard costs adds ~$200k to phase 1. Say which story you are telling FirstBank.

Kitchen + baths

Biggest finish swing. Semi-custom is in the mid. True custom kitchen alone can eat the contingency.

Drawings already exist

Pence Keystone set is the lift. You are not buying a custom architect from zero. That is why pre-con is small.

Bank conversation — MC recommendation

Ask construction-to-perm on phase 1 $1.2M with a $200k contingency / well reserve so the note can live at $1.4M if the well and a GC show up. Down payment: mountain construction-to-perm is typically 20–30% cash or equity in the land. On $1.4M that is $280–420k down, or lever a cash-paid building as you already said. Interest reserve for 16 months at 7.5% on a $1.1M average drawn balance is roughly $110k — put it in the loan, do not pretend draws are free.
