Section 4
Commission · 2% of overall sales

You will receive a commission equal to two percent (2%) of Company gross sales (all revenue, not limited to sales you personally close). This commission is payable on a project only if actual gross profit margin on that project is at least thirty-seven percent (37%). If actual gross profit margin on a project is below 37%, no 2% is due on that project's revenue.

Gross profit margin means (Sale minus Direct cost of logs and materials minus Direct project labor) divided by Sale.

The Company uses the following planning allocation to underwrite a job. This is a target stack, not a guarantee of actual costs:

Planning stackShare of sale

Gross profit margin target / gate37%
