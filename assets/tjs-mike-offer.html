Commission

Two percent (2%) of total company gross sales.

Payable on a project only if actual gross profit margin on that project is 37% or greater.

Gross profit margin means sale minus direct cost of logs and materials minus direct project labor, divided by sale.

Profit share

Of net operating income, TJ's allocates 20% to philanthropy, 20% to reinvestment, and 60% to the people pool.

You receive 10% of that 60% people pool as a W-2 bonus. On $100 of net operating income, that is $6. If there is no positive net operating income, the bonus is $0.

Benefits and time off

  • No company medical, dental, or vision. You have declined those benefits.
  • Up to three weeks of vacation a year, scheduled so coverage is maintained.
  • The office runs a six-day workweek across the team. That is the team cadence. It is not a requirement that you work six days.

Employment is at-will under Colorado law. Either of us may end it at any time.
