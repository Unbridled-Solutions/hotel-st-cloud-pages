How we go global without becoming one company.

Follow-up to the OrangeDoor Europe SWOT and the May Unbridled competitive SWOT. Positioning, plans, and lines you can actually say in a room. Built so each house keeps its name, its book, and its pride.

August 2026 · Companion to orangedoor-swot and the May 2026 Unbridled SWOT. Not a rebrand. Not a public site.

The call

Endorsed house. Not a branded house.

Do not fold OrangeDoor into Unbridled. Do not hide Unbridled behind OrangeDoor. Sell one operating system and keep two public names. The lockup is already right: OrangeDoor, an Unbridled company. That is the architecture. Everything else is execution.

What both SWOTs already said

OrangeDoor · August 2026 Stop selling “European agency.”

Quiet brand. London-heavy Europe. Merger unfinished in the room. The year is US clients who need Europe and EU clients who need the States. One team. One invoice. No third-party “local partner.”

Unbridled · May 2026 The ecosystem is the moat. Nobody can see it.

Five companies beat Opus, Bishop-McCann, and M&IW on integration. We lose on LinkedIn, logo theater, a named method, GIFT'D visibility, and a clear global sentence. OrangeDoor is the missing geography line. Do not spend it by renaming everyone.

Positioning

We are the transatlantic independent. Mid-market and upper-mid buyers who want one team on both sides of the ocean, and still want a human on the phone. We are not MCI. We do not need to be.

For the buyer: one show lead, one invoice, one standard. London exhibitions and strategy. US production, media, travel, and gifts. Purpose you can audit: B Corp 104, plus 20/20/60.

For our people: you did not get acquired into a logo. You gained a peer with the same values and a bigger bench.

Four story pillars

1 · Kindred, not conquered

Stan and Andrew on the J&J account. The Shoreditch hillside. Amy in a hallway. Elizabeth and Stan: same values, an ocean apart. Use this once, then prove it with a shipped job, not a myth.

2 · The bench, named

OrangeDoor: tech, finance, HR, exhibitions. Unbridled: pharma, food and beverage, big-room production, media, travel, GIFT'D. Five US offices plus London. Say the lanes. Stop saying “full service.”

3 · One show system

May SWOT said name the method. August SWOT said hide the seam. Same job. Package how we run a dual-continent show. Put it in every deck. If the client feels two agencies, we lose.

4 · Purpose with a receipt

B Corp + 20/20/60 + UnbridledACTS. Values-economy buyers (the May opportunity) and EU sustainability committees (the August opportunity) are the same person with two passports.

Six marketing plans

Plan 1 · Shared sales kit · 30 days · Stan + Elizabeth + Scott

One leave-behind. Two covers.

  • A 12-page joint capability PDF and a matching HTML page. Cover A leads OrangeDoor. Cover B leads Unbridled. Same middle: people, lanes, offices, 20/20/60, three proof jobs.
  • Proof jobs: the New England show already shipped together, one OrangeDoor EU exhibition, one Unbridled US production. Names only with permission.
  • A one-page “how we staff a transatlantic show” diagram. This is the anti-seam asset. If we cannot draw it, we are not ready to sell it.
  • Rate card logic stays local. The kit never pretends there is one global price list.
Plan 2 · The only list that matters · 90 days · commercial leads both sides

Ten named dual-continent accounts.

  • Five from the OrangeDoor book that already buy or want the US. Five from the Unbridled book that already buy or want Europe.
  • Each account gets one owner, one backup on the other shore, and one 2026 offer: “next show, we run both sides.”
  • Do not open Middle East or Asia offices. Follow two flagship clients if they pay for year one. That is still the SWOT rule.
  • Success is not awareness. Success is two signed dual-continent SOWs and a reuse story.
Plan 3 · LinkedIn is the street · ongoing · marketing both houses

One editorial calendar. Separate voices.

  • May SWOT: M&IW 31k, Opus 26k, us fragmented. Fix cadence, not a mega-brand.
  • Unbridled LinkedIn: Stan, production floors, 20/20/60, US client stories, “now we answer in London.”
  • OrangeDoor LinkedIn: Elizabeth, exhibitions, B Corp, tech/finance/HR, “an Unbridled company” in the about line and footer, not in every caption.
  • Once a week, a joint post with both logos and a real job or a real person. Never a “synergy” graphic.
  • GIFT'D, Productions, Media, Travel keep their own pages. They do not become OrangeDoor channels.
Plan 4 · Sites and lockups · 45 days · marketing + web

Fix the sentence. Do not rebuild the house.

  • weareorangedoor.com: add the lockup, five US cities, Unbridled Productions / Media / Travel / GIFT'D as sister capabilities, not sub-brands.
  • unbridled.com: London is already in the footer. Promote it. A short “OrangeDoor, an Unbridled company” page. No UK rebrand of the US site.
  • Both sites: one shared “work with us across the Atlantic” form that routes to a human, not a black hole.
  • Directory and email: “OrangeDoor, an Unbridled company.” Never “Orange Door.” Never “Orange Store.”
Plan 5 · Purpose as a sales channel · 90 days · Elizabeth + Meg + Stan

Sell the receipt, not the sermon.

  • One joint purpose sheet: B Corp score, 20/20/60 in the canonical wording, UnbridledACTS link, majority-female leadership at OrangeDoor.
  • Target list: B Corps, employee-owned firms, and EU buyers with a sustainability gate. This is the May “values economy” play with a London door on it.
  • Do not let holding companies copy the line. They can rent the words. They cannot show the model.
Plan 6 · Name the method · 60 days · ops + show leads

Give the operating system a short name, then shut up about branding it.

  • May SWOT wanted a named method so we stop losing to JOY Index and ISG. August SWOT wanted the seam gone. Same document.
  • Working title only (pick one and live with it): Show One or One Call. It is a staffing and handoff standard, not a product launch.
  • One page: who owns the room, who owns the booth, who owns travel, who owns the cut, what the client sees. Put it in the kit from Plan 1.
  • If London and Denver cannot run a rehearsal on the same document, do not put the line on a website yet.

90 days

  1. Days 1–14. Lock the architecture in writing with Elizabeth and Andrew. Approve the master line and the lockup rule. Name the ten accounts.
  2. Days 15–45. Ship the two-cover sales kit, the site sentences, LinkedIn about lines, and the purpose sheet.
  3. Days 46–90. Run the ten-account motion. Publish two joint proof stories. Rehearse Show One on a live job. Decide Middle East only if a client funds it.

Autonomy test

Before any asset ships, ask four questions. If any answer is no, it does not leave the building.

  • Can OrangeDoor still introduce itself without saying Unbridled first?
  • Can Unbridled Productions still sell a US show without a London footnote?
  • Would Elizabeth be proud to hang this in Bromley?
  • Would Stan be proud to hang this in Denver?

What I would not do

I would not launch a third brand. I would not run a “now we are global” ad campaign. I would not merge LinkedIn pages. I would not put OrangeDoor on Hotel St. Cloud or Fremont Makers. Those are a different family. Live events only.

I would sell the ten accounts, publish the lockup, and make the seam disappear in the room. Awareness follows work. It does not lead it.
