How you use it now

Open the quote. Status is four radios: Proposed, Contracted, Completed, Lost.

To leave Proposed, tap Issue Contract or pick Contracted on the Status dropdown in All Quotes. Set the install date. The system fills a 90-day deposit window: 50% today, 30% in 45 days, 20% at install. Password 5476 locks it and opens the contract packet: agreement, first deposit invoice, deposit schedule, quote.

Print Invoice on a contracted job names the amount due this invoice and the install date.

Deposits Due is the rolling 12-month receivable. Unpaid deposits populate the month they are due. Pipeline is the board. On a contracted job: Open quote, Open contract (the packet), Completed.
