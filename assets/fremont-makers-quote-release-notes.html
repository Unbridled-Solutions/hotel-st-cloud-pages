How you use it now

Open the quote. Status is four radios: Proposed, Contracted, Completed, Lost.

To leave Proposed, tap Issue Contract or pick Contracted on the Status dropdown in All Quotes. Set the install date. The system fills a 90-day deposit window: 50% today, 30% in 45 days, 20% at install. Password 5476 locks it and opens the contract packet: filled buyer fields, invoice total, deposit dates and amounts, plus the Sep 2 terms. Print, download Word, or send for signature. Dollars stay on the quote.

Print Invoice uses the quote you have open. If that quote is contracted, the invoice names the next unpaid deposit and its due date.

Deposits Due is the rolling receivable. Pipeline is the board. On a contracted job: Open quote, Open contract (the packet), Completed.
