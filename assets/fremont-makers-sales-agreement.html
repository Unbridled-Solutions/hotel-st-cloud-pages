Section 2
What This Agreement Covers

This Agreement plus Seller's written Quote and/or Invoice together form the entire deal. The Quote and Invoice are part of this Agreement. Dollar amounts live only on the Quote or Invoice. Pricing is by bid. This form is the terms.

Section 3
What Seller Sells

Seller sells ready-to-assemble (RTA / flat-pack) cabinets and related hardware listed on the Quote or Invoice.

Unless the Quote says otherwise in writing, Seller does not provide design professional services, field measuring, installation, plumbing, electrical work, countertops, or appliances.

Section 4
Reseller and Origin

Seller is a reseller, not the manufacturer. Cabinets come as ready-to-assemble flat packs, shipped to Cañon City, Colorado.

Most items are available from warehouses in the United States. Custom or out-of-stock orders may take longer because some products originate overseas. Lead times are estimates. Delays in manufacturing, freight, or trucking are not a breach if Seller keeps Buyer reasonably informed.

Section 5
Quote, Invoice, and Changes

If the Quote and Invoice conflict, the Invoice controls price after Buyer approval. This Agreement controls all other terms.

Changes are valid only in a written change order signed by both parties. If manufacturer price, tariffs, or extra freight move after the Quote but before the factory order is placed, Seller may revise the Quote before the 50% payment is accepted. After the factory order is placed, Buyer pays documented extras only if Buyer approved the change in writing.

Section 7
Measurements and Specifications

Buyer is solely responsible for measurements, quantities, layouts, and specifications. Seller is not liable if Buyer's measurements or specifications are wrong.

Wrong sizes, missing fillers, incorrect appliance openings, or similar issues are Buyer's cost to correct, including reorder, restocking, and freight. Seller may review drawings as a courtesy. That review is not a warranty that the Goods will fit.

Section 8
Cancellation and Returns

Once the factory order is placed, special-order RTA cabinets are generally not returnable. If cancellation is allowed at all, the deposit is applied to restocking, freight, and factory charges. Unused stock hardware may be returned only if Seller agrees in writing in advance.

Section 9
Delivery, Pickup, and Inspection

Buyer inspects the Goods at pickup or delivery and notes visible damage on the bill of lading or delivery ticket. Failure to note damage within 48 hours means the Goods are deemed accepted, except for hidden (latent) defects.

Risk of loss passes to Buyer on pickup, or on delivery to the site address. Title passes only when Buyer has paid in full.

Section 10
Assembly

The Goods are flat-pack. Assembly is by Buyer or Buyer's contractor unless the Quote expressly includes Seller assembly. If Seller assembly is included, it is assembly only. It is not installation into the building.

Section 11
Warranty and Limit of Liability

Seller passes through any manufacturer warranty that applies. Seller warrants it has the right to sell the Goods. Except for that, Seller makes no other warranties.

To the extent Colorado law allows, Seller disclaims implied warranties of merchantability and fitness for a particular purpose. This is written to cover builders, contractors, and homeowners. A Colorado attorney should confirm how it treats commercial sales versus consumer sales.

Seller's total liability under this Agreement is limited to the amounts Buyer actually paid for the affected Goods. Seller is not liable for consequential, delay, lost-rent, lost-profit, or similar damages.

Section 12
Events Outside Seller's Control

Seller is not liable for delays or failures caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including factory issues, ocean freight, customs, weather, labor disputes, or government actions, provided Seller notifies Buyer promptly.

Section 13
Entire Agreement

This Agreement, together with the Quote and Invoice, is the entire agreement. It replaces prior discussions. If any part is unenforceable, the rest stays. Failure to enforce a term is not a waiver. Signatures may be wet-ink or electronic under the Colorado Uniform Electronic Transactions Act.

Section 14
Colorado Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Colorado, without regard to conflict-of-laws rules.

Section 15
Arbitration

Any dispute under this Agreement will be resolved by binding arbitration in Fremont County, Colorado, under the Colorado Uniform Arbitration Act (C.R.S. § 13-22-201 et seq.). One arbitrator. Each side pays its own attorney fees unless the arbitrator awards otherwise.

Either party may use small claims court if the amount is within that court's limit. If arbitration is unavailable, venue is the state courts of Fremont County, Colorado.
