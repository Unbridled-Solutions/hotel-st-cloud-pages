The agency, not the tokens

What BeUnbridled sells when models are Grok, Anthropic, and whatever is cheap next year. Digital chiefs of staff. White glove. A group channel for people who need the CoS and must not own it. Consulting that builds the same thing inside a company’s existing software. Companion to the OpCo group playbook.

The business We are a multi-model agent agency. We do not sell a chatbot seat. We install a named chief of staff on a private box, teach it the company, and stay on the wire. Tokens get cheaper. That is why you do not monetize tokens. You monetize judgment, DNA, permissioning, and the room where the rest of the company can talk without getting the keys. Room to be human is the product. The agent does the machine work.

What we actually sell

Three offers, one firm

  • Digital chief of staff. Dedicated agent, short allow list, company DNA, own bot, own memory. Fremont, Timber, Grandpa Vaughan are the proof, not a metaphor.
  • White glove. We provision, we write the soul, we hold deploy, we fix it at 7am. The client’s principals talk to the CoS. They do not SSH.
  • Floor channel. A group where staff ask and flag. Combined knowledge of the company CoS plus our orchestrator when software is in play. Information seats, not admin seats.

On the side, and this is the long game: agentic consulting. Help a company design the same pattern inside their enterprise stack (Microsoft, Google, their ERP) and outside it (the sidecar CoS they actually message). Same playbook. Different wiring.

Why this holds when inference is cheap

  • Anyone can open ChatGPT. Almost nobody will name who is allowed to ship, who is allowed to ask, and what the agent must never delete.
  • The scarce work is DNA, tools, allow lists, and “this is not ready.” That does not go to zero with token price.
  • A floor group without a software gate becomes shadow IT in a week. We sell the gate.
  • Multi-model is a feature of the agency, not the pitch. Grok, Anthropic, open weights: we pick for the job. The client buys the CoS, not a model card.

How to talk about price without being a token shop

Lead with the CoS and the room. “You get a named chief of staff and a channel your people can use.” Tokens are a utility line, like electricity. Consulting is how their quote builder, their CRM, their Monday board actually move. If a prospect wants “unlimited ChatGPT for the staff,” that is not this firm.

Who it is for

  • Owner-operated companies that already have a human who is drowning in the middle (ops, quotes, job packets, follow-up).
  • A second brand or shop that should not share a brain with the first (Fremont is not Timber is not hospitality).
  • A team that needs the agent in the group chat, and an owner who still holds production.

Who it is not for

  • A company that wants one shared bot for 80 people and no adult in the room.
  • Regulated work that must sit on its own box with its own lawyer (wealth, medical, anything that cannot share a disk). That is still a dedicated server, still white glove, not a group add-on.
  • A prospect whose only ask is “cheaper tokens.” Send them to a model host.

How a deal should run

  1. DNA week. Who they serve, what must never happen, tools, the one thing that would change the week.
  2. CoS live. Principals only. First week is DMs. Prove it knows the shop.
  3. Floor channel. One group, company bot as voice, our orchestrator for software, named information seats. Paste the day-one script from the playbook.
  4. First build. One tool they already feel. Quote, tracker, packet. Live URL. Stop there until they use it.
  5. Enterprise pass, only if asked. Wire the same roles into their Microsoft or Google tenant. Do not lead with this. It is how you stay when they already have IT.

What we never promise

  • That two bots in a group will “talk to each other.” They will not. The thread is the shared brain.
  • That floor users can approve production. If we sell that, we inherit their mess.
  • That the CoS replaces their ERP, PMS, or accounting system. It sits on top and next to.
  • That model choice is the product. Grok vs Anthropic is our problem.

One sentence for a room

BeUnbridled installs a digital chief of staff for the people who run the company, then a group channel for everyone else, and stays on as the agency that builds the tools and holds the keys. Tokens are included. The judgment is the fee.
