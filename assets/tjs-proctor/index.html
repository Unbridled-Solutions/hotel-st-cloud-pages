Mary Drive build. My recommendation.

Stan Bullis  ·  4 Sep 2026  ·  Unbridled Holdings X

This is my recommendation for the Mary Drive build. The Keystone is a product TJ's already knows how to build. We have the architectural drawings. Minimal lift.

The garage and the house sit well on this land. If you are looking at the house from Mary Drive, and we are not sure we can use that entrance yet, you would see the house with the garage on the right. That garage blocks the neighbor's deck. They built in an odd place. The land falls off toward the gully. That is perfect for the Keystone walk-out.

If we have to come in from Annie Drive, we flip the garage doors to the back. The garage is its own building with a breezeway. We have options either way.

Start as a main and upper house. Frame the basement. Put the electrical and the plumbing down there. About 3,000 square feet. At $400 a square foot, I think we build that for $1.2 million or less.

At that number we could rent it on Airbnb. Jim, you have a better read on that than I do. If we could not sell it, we could keep it in Holdings X. Friends and family. An extra house.

If times are good, we finish the basement. Then you have a 5,000 square foot house, equally rentable. Still about $400 a square foot. That is about $2 million. That is the upper end of where we want to be. At that number we might just keep it. We could also sell it, or pre-sell it, with the option to move the floor plan around.

So: a 3,000 square foot model with the option to build out the basement. Room to play with the build and the numbers. It would look right on that lot. Doable on construction. Doable on timing.

FirstBank is interested in pulling the loan. They know we would lever against the buildings we have already paid cash for. We will have to come up with a down payment. Construction-to-perm. While we build, we only pay interest on the money we are using. When we are done, it converts to perm.

The pictures here are not carved in stone. I was playing with this to see if we could put a package in front of a customer that is actually interesting. That only works if we have a digital copy of the plans. TJ's, if we have that digital set, we can make a real website for the customer.

Hope you enjoy it. We will pick this up after you have looked.

Stan
