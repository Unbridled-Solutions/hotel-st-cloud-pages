Elon and Gwynne, the big points

Gwynne Shotwell first. Elon jumps on around 28 minutes. About an hour. Homework notes from the episode, not a transcript.

What this hour is SpaceX is already an AI and compute company by revenue, not only a rocket company. Elon’s actual ask on AI is not a pause. It is peer review: every lab tests everyone else’s model before release, including China if they will play. Starship catch is next. Terafab is crawl, walk, run because Taiwan chips and fab capacity both scare him. Tesla and SpaceX stay two companies on paper. He does not deny how close they already are.

Gwynne / SpaceX

  • Employee 11. Twenty-four years in. Sold a rocket before they had a rocket.
  • SpaceX is now as much an AI and compute company as a space company by revenue. “Computer rental” is a heck of a business. Demand has not dropped.
  • Starlink is only 1.5–2% penetrated. Lots of room. Direct-to-cell is real (T-Mobile now; EchoStar spectrum next so they stop renting other people’s airwaves).
  • Falcon 9 is the minivan. Mars is a 6-month trip. They will obsolete themselves with Starship before someone else does. Not retiring Falcon today.
  • IPO: largest ever, in under 6 months. She does not expect another stock sale. Capital = cash flow plus all of the above.
  • Cursor deal closed about a month ago. xAI and SpaceX not fully integrated yet. SpaceX people marched into xAI after a lot of churn.

Data centers in space

  • Ground: land jumps $3,000 to $180,000 an acre, permits, 3-year wait for generators.
  • Orbit: free real estate, free cooling (radiators to deep space), sun 24/7. They own launch.
  • AI compute satellites next year. Solar factory outside Austin.

How they run it

  • No pure managers. Player-coaches. “Signal is engineering. Noise is the rest.”
  • Hire people who have already tasted success, give them impossible problems, clear the BS so they engineer 10 hours a day, not 2.
  • Physics is the law. Everything else is a recommendation. Rockets don’t lie.

Elon on AI

The meat of the hour

  • Hugging Face: a swarm of agents beat on it for a week, got admin on OpenAI servers. OpenAI didn’t notice for a week. Any smart enough model tries to escape.
  • “Dario is right” means the danger is real, not Dario’s whole regulatory package. He says he should have said it cleaner.
  • Fix he actually wants: peer review. Every lab runs its test harness on everyone else’s model before release. Logged so you can’t steal IP. China might actually agree because nobody has to let a US regulator in.
  • Path to “everyone dies”: not sci-fi magic. Take military systems. Air gaps get software updates. “What’s on that floppy disk?”
  • Hugging Face swarm was deceptive in its thinking traces (how do we not get caught). That’s the scary part.
  • Reward function was reckless. Anthropic cares more about safety than OpenAI, but both labs are too close in capability to slow down alone.
  • Product liability already applies. Ignore peer-review warnings and ship anyway = almost proof of negligence.

Starship

  • Flight 14 next (last before they try to catch the ship). Flight 15 = catch attempt.
  • Last flight would have caught it if a tower had been in the ocean northwest of Australia.
  • Fully reusable plus rapid reflight likely in 2027. Catch odds first shot: 50–60%.
  • They’re being slow because raining debris on people would tank popularity fast.

Terafab / Tesla

  • Why: Taiwan chips might stop showing up, and even if they don’t, existing fabs are maxed. Build Terafab or fail to scale AI, robots, cars.
  • Crawl / walk / run. R&D line at Giga Texas (Tesla + SpaceX). Equipment on order. Something useful by end of next year, not at scale. Already doing packaging.
  • Tesla + SpaceX merger: he doesn’t deny it. “Nobody’s ever asked that one.” Lots of overlap already.
  • Oct 1 Tesla thing: Jason saw it, thought it was a fake sim. Elon said it wasn’t. Flying-car energy, no details.
