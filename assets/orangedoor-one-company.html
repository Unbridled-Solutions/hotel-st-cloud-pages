Can we become one company?

Straight answer from the public record, the two SWOTs, and how US-UK agency deals actually work. Not a valuation. Not legal advice. A decision brief.

Drafted by MC as a starting point. Not reviewed by a licensed attorney in Colorado or England and Wales. Do not treat this as a legal opinion, tax opinion, or a description of the signed deal. The SPA is not in this vault. Before anyone files, signs, or tells a client we are one legal entity, a US and a UK lawyer need to read the actual documents.
The call

We are already one group. We are not one company. We should not try to be.

One company in the market: yes, for dual-continent work. One company on a shared board and a shared profit model: already started. One surviving legal entity that employs everyone from Denver to Bromley: not set up, not how this deal was sold, and not worth the wreckage. Keep two OpCos. Tighten the HoldCo. Sell as one team.

Four meanings of “one company”

Meaning Status Should we?
Market face. One team, one invoice, one show lead on dual-continent work. Sold. Not finished in the room. Yes. This is the year.
Governance. Shared board, shared leadership, shared 20/20/60. Started 7 Nov 2025. Yes. Finish it. Write the reserved matters.
Group. One HoldCo owns both OpCos. Two legal employers. Two VAT / tax homes. Intended. Cap table not public-clean. Yes. This is the real “one company.”
Single legal entity. Dissolve HAMG Ltd or Unbridled Solutions LLC into the other. One employer, one contract paper, one name at Companies House and the Colorado SOS. Not done. Not how US-UK private deals work. No.

Why a true legal collapse is a bad idea

You cannot cleanly merge a Colorado LLC into a UK limited company the way two Delaware corps merge. Cross-border statutory merger is the wrong tool for a 200-person agency. The grown-up structure is always: HoldCo on top, local OpCo in each country.

What breaks if you force one legal entity
  • UK employment contracts, PAYE, pensions, TUPE if you move people.
  • US employment, payroll tax, workers’ comp, at-will vs UK unfair dismissal.
  • VAT, corporation tax, permanent establishment. A Denver LLC trading hard in London is a gift to HMRC.
  • Client MSAs, insurance, PI, venue contracts, data processing. Every paper has a named company on it.
  • B Corp sits on the UK company (score 104). Smash the entity and you reopen certification.
  • Banking, charges, and any residual UK security. You just finished cleaning one charge.
What you do not gain
  • Procurement already buys “one invoice.” A services agreement between sister companies does that. You do not need one legal body.
  • Brand already has a lockup. Renaming everyone Unbridled UK is a takeover story. Elizabeth said that is not the deal.
  • The May SWOT moat is the ecosystem of named companies. Collapse Live Events into one logo and you copy Opus. We lose the thing they cannot buy.
  • Hotel St. Cloud, Fremont Makers, Wealth, Holdings stay out. “One Unbridled company” for the whole family office is a different, worse conversation.

What “one company” should mean here

Live Events HoldCo. Unbridled Companies, or a dedicated Live Events holding company, owns Unbridled Solutions LLC and Heron Anstead Media Global Ltd (OrangeDoor). Productions, Media, Travel, and GIFT'D stay sister companies, not departments of London.

Two OpCos forever. US work contracts through the US company. UK and most EU work contracts through the UK company. Dual-continent jobs: one commercial lead, intercompany subcontract, one client invoice from the lead OpCo.

One commercial motion. Shared show operating system. Shared 20/20/60 on Live Events profit, with the 60% going to the people and partners, not “employees” as a slogan. Shared reserved matters: new offices, debt, equity, brand changes, hiring of C-level, anything over an agreed dollar and pound gate.

If we did push toward one company anyway

  1. Confirm the cap table. Get the signed SPA, the new articles, and a current PSC filing. If Elizabeth and Richard still own the UK company on the register, we are not one company in any legal sense. We are a partnership with a shared board.
  2. Do not dissolve HAMG Ltd. If Unbridled does not yet own it, finish the share purchase or share-for-share into the HoldCo. Leave the UK company standing.
  3. Optional rename only. “OrangeDoor Limited, an Unbridled company” as a trading style is already enough. A legal name change to Unbridled UK Ltd is a signal of takeover. I would not do it in year one.
  4. One MSA addendum. A short “we may deliver through either OpCo” clause for new dual-continent clients. That is the invoice problem, solved without a merger statute.
  5. Employment stays local. Nobody in Bromley should get a Colorado offer letter. Nobody in Denver should go on a UK contract unless they actually move.

Culture test (this is the real risk)

Yesterday’s SWOT said the deal dies in 18 months if London feels acquired or Denver treats London as a satellite. “We are now one company” is the sentence that makes both things true at once. Elizabeth’s public line was the opposite: partnership, identity kept, merger of mindsets.

Stan already lived the other movie. Selling 51% and outsourcing identity took ten years to buy back. Do not do that to OrangeDoor, and do not do it to Unbridled by swallowing a 25-year UK name for the comfort of a single letterhead.

Recommendation

  • Become one group, visibly. HoldCo. Shared board. Shared 20/20/60. One show system. One invoice for the client.
  • Stay two companies on paper. US OpCo. UK OpCo. Two brands, endorsed, not erased.
  • Do not file a statutory merger. Do not wind up HAMG Ltd to look tidy. Do not rebrand OrangeDoor as Unbridled UK this year.
  • Next concrete step: put the SPA and current cap table in front of Colorado counsel and English counsel, then update the PSC if the register is stale. Until that packet exists, “one company” is a press sentence, not a fact.
