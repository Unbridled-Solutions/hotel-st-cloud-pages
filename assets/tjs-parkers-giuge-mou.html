In this Memorandum, "TJ's" means TJ's Log & Timber, "Parker's" means Colorado Sports Academy, Inc. doing business as Parker's Platoon, and "GIUGE" means GIUGE, LLC. Together they are the "Parties."

Section 2
Purpose

This Memorandum of Understanding records the Parties' shared understanding of a three-part arrangement for log cabin, log railing, and log accessory sales in Grand Lake, Colorado:

  • TJ's will occupy front-lot display space on Parker's property to show a sample log cabin, log railing, and log accessories.
  • GIUGE will act as a sales office and selling agent for TJ's Qualifying Products, paid by commission.
  • If GIUGE is also the general contractor on a sale it helps close, the commission is dropped and GIUGE is paid only through its GC contract.

The Parties intend to formalize these terms, if they proceed, in a short-term occupancy or license agreement, a sales-agency or independent-contractor agreement, and any related insurance certificates prepared by legal counsel.

Section 3
Defined Terms
  • Qualifying Products means log cabins, log railing, and log accessories sold by TJ's.
  • Display Area means the front lot on Parker's Grand Lake property, used only to display a sample log cabin, railing, and accessories. Exact legal description and site sketch to be attached as Exhibit A.
  • Covered Sale means a closed sale of Qualifying Products in which GIUGE helped TJ's close the sale or acted as selling agent.
  • Sale Amount means the total contract price of Qualifying Products on TJ's invoice to the customer, before sales tax and before unrelated third-party charges. Example: a $100,000 cabin is a $100,000 Sale Amount.
  • Direct GC Sale means a Covered Sale on which GIUGE is also engaged as general contractor for the same customer and project.

Section 5

GIUGE will act as a sales office and selling agent for TJ's Qualifying Products. Any log cabin, log railing, or log accessory sale that results from GIUGE helping TJ's close the sale, or from GIUGE acting as selling agent, is a Covered Sale.

Example Commission
  • Commission: three percent (3%) of the Sale Amount, paid by TJ's to GIUGE.
  • Worked example: $100,000 cabin sale = $3,000 commission.
  • Same 3% applies to log railing and log accessory sales that are Covered Sales, not only full cabins.
  • Commission is earned only when the customer has a signed purchase contract with TJ's and TJ's has received the deposit or payment milestone counsel later specifies. Suggested default: earned on TJ's receipt of the customer's first cleared payment, payable within 15 days after that receipt.
  • GIUGE is an independent contractor, not an employee of TJ's or Parker's, and not a real-estate broker under this Memorandum unless GIUGE separately holds and uses a required license. Counsel must confirm whether any sale of a cabin kit or installed home requires a Colorado real-estate license.
  • This is not an exclusive listing. TJ's may sell through other channels. Only Covered Sales earn the 3%.
Section 6
Part Three · Direct GC Sales · Commission Forfeited

GIUGE is a qualified general contractor. If GIUGE sells a log cabin (or other Qualifying Products) on which GIUGE is also the GC, the 3% commission is excluded. That compensation is built into GIUGE's GC contract markup instead.

  • The forfeiture applies to the entire Covered Sale, not a reduced rate. There is no 3% plus GC markup on the same job.
  • If GIUGE later becomes GC after a commission was already paid, GIUGE will refund the commission to TJ's within 15 days of signing the GC contract, unless the Parties agree in writing to a different true-up.
  • Parker's does not receive a share of commission or GC markup under this Memorandum.
Section 7
Insurance, Care, and Removal
Suggested terms · confirm with counsel and carriers
  • TJ's keeps title to all display goods and carries property insurance on them.
  • TJ's carries commercial general liability covering the Display Area during the term, with Parker's named as additional insured. Suggested floor: $1,000,000 per occurrence.
  • GIUGE carries its own contractor liability and any license bonds required for GC work. Those policies are separate from this display arrangement.
  • TJ's restores the Display Area at the end of the term, ordinary wear excepted, and removes the sample cabin, railing, and accessories unless a winter extension is agreed in writing.
Section 8
Term, Winter Review, and Ending Early
  • Display occupancy: September 1, 2026 through October 31, 2026.
  • Sales-agency understanding: begins September 1, 2026 and continues until a Party gives 15 days' written notice, or until replaced by a formal agreement.
  • Winter review: on or before October 15, 2026, the Parties meet and decide whether the display stays, comes down, or is winterized, and on what rent if it stays.
  • Any Party may walk away from this non-binding Memorandum at any time before formal documents are signed. Display goods still come off the lot on reasonable notice if occupancy ends.
Section 9
What This Memorandum Does Not Do
  • It does not transfer any ownership of Parker's real property.
  • It does not make any Party a partner, joint venturer, or employee of another Party.
  • It does not give GIUGE authority to bind TJ's on price, discounts, or contracts unless TJ's later gives that authority in writing.
  • It does not create a listing, brokerage, or exclusive territory.
  • It does not require Parker's to endorse any product or share donor or member lists.
