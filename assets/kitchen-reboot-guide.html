⚡ Power Outage — Kitchen Reboot Sequence
⚠️
Wait until power is fully restored before starting. Tell the manager you're beginning the reboot.
  1. Hood vent control panel — find it on the ceiling above the cooking line toward the back of the kitchen (mounted on the underside of the hood). Press the top right button to open gas lines to the kitchen.
  2. Check each appliance: confirm the green gas handle behind it is parallel to the pipe (= ON).
  3. Light pilots on each appliance — see appliance cards below.
  4. Confirm all appliances are lit and operational, then notify the manager.
🔥 Grill
Light each section individually using a lighter. Hold near each pilot point and confirm ignition across the full grill surface.
✓ Blue flame visible through slot on each section
🍳 Stovetop
Pilots are under the grates — accessible from the front. Insert lighter through the hole under each grate.
✓ Blue flame visible through hole under grate
🍟 Fryer
The pilot light indicator is the blue light on the front face of the unit. If not lit, reignite at the pilot location on the unit.
✓ Blue indicator light ON before use
🔥 Gas Shutoff — Individual Appliances

Each appliance has a green handle located behind it. Turn until the handle stops — perpendicular to the pipe = OFF.

Grill
Green handle behind grill, right side
⊥ = OFF
Stovetop
Green handle behind stovetop unit
⊥ = OFF
Fryer
Green handle behind fryer unit
⊥ = OFF
🚨
Gas leak confirmed in kitchen? Shut off valves → do NOT flip switches → evacuate → call 911 → notify manager.
✅ Post-Reboot Checklist
  • Hood vent GAS ON button pressed — gas lines open to kitchen
  • Grill: all sections lit, blue flame visible through slot
  • Stovetop: all pilots lit, blue flame visible under grates
  • Fryer: blue pilot indicator light confirmed ON
  • All green gas handles confirmed parallel to pipe (ON)
  • Manager on duty notified — reboot complete
